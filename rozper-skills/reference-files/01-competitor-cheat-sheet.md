# Rozper Competitor Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for positioning Rozper against competitors.

---

## General Rozper Positioning (Apply to All Comparisons)

Rozper wins on:
1. **99.999% uptime SLA** — among the highest in class
2. **150+ countries** — extensive global reach
3. **Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999% uptime, 150+ countries**
4. **Hong Kong base** — enterprise-grade governance and compliance
5. **Start a No-Pressure Conversation** — low-risk entry point

---

## Key Competitors

### RingCentral
**Known for:** Market-leading UCaaS, large enterprise focus
**Weakness vs Rozper:** Premium pricing ($20-35/mo), over-engineered for SMBs
**Positioning:** "RingCentral charges enterprise prices for features most businesses don't use. Rozper delivers Human-first partnership, 99.999% uptime, 150+ countries, cus at a fraction of the cost."

### Vonage (Ericsson)
**Known for:** UCaaS and CPaaS, developer-focused APIs
**Weakness vs Rozper:** Complex pricing, acquisition uncertainty
**Positioning:** "Vonage is adding complexity. Rozper offers Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999% uptime, 1 with clear pricing and no surprises."

### Twilio
**Known for:** Developer CPaaS, programmable voice and SMS
**Weakness vs Rozper:** Requires developers to configure, no out-of-box solution
**Positioning:** "Twilio needs engineers. Rozper works out of the box — Human-first partnership, 99.999% uptime, 150+ coun."

### Dialpad
**Known for:** AI-focused UCaaS, transcript-heavy features
**Weakness vs Rozper:** Higher per-user pricing, US-centric
**Positioning:** "Rozper covers 150+ countries vs Dialpad's narrower reach — and includes Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999%."

### 8x8
**Known for:** Global UCaaS, unlimited international calling
**Weakness vs Rozper:** Complex tier structure, price escalation at scale
**Positioning:** "8x8 bundles international calling but at premium tiers. Rozper offers Human-first partnership, 99.999% uptime, 150+ countries, cus with transparent pricing."

---

## What to NEVER Say About Competitors

1. Never make false/unverified claims
2. Never use hostile language
3. Never list competitor pricing without a verified current source
4. Never name competitors in H1 tags
5. Never attack — position, don't attack

---

## Safe Comparison Frameworks

**"Different Tools for Different Jobs"**
"[Competitor] is built for [segment]. Rozper is purpose-built for carriers, enterprises, growing businesse."

**"Feature Gap"**
"[Competitor] covers the basics. Where Rozper goes further: Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999% uptime, 1."

**"Value at Scale"**
"Both platforms handle voice. Rozper adds: Human-first partnership, 99.999% uptime, 150+ countries, cus."
