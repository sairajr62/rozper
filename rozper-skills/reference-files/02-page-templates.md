# Rozper Page Templates

Scaffolds for every major Rozper page type. Replace ALL bracketed content with original copy. NEVER reuse scaffold language across pages.

---

```

URL: /[keyword-slug]/

H1: [6-10 words — outcome or bold claim]
Subheadline: [Who it's for + what they get]

Trust: "99.999% Uptime  |  150+ Countries  |  24/7 Expert Support  |  Risk-Free Trial"

[2-3 sentences — specific pain, not generic]

H2: [Rozper as the solution]
• [Feature 1] — [specific benefit]
• [Feature 2] — [specific benefit]
• [Feature 3] — [specific benefit]
• [Feature 4] — [specific benefit]

[Mid-page CTA]

H2: [Unique Rozper differentiator section]
[2-3 sentences on Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999% uptime, 1]

H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Q: [Question with primary keyword]
A: [2-3 sentence direct answer]
[Repeat for 4-5 questions]

[Final CTA Block]
```

```
URL: /blog/[keyword-slug]/

H1: [Blog title — informational, helpful]

[Opening hook — 1-3 sentences. Stat / Question / Bold claim / Scenario / Challenge]

[Context paragraph — why this topic matters now]

H2: [First major section]
[3-5 sentences with data point]

H2: [Second major section]
[3-5 sentences]

[Continue 4-7 H2 sections]

H2: [Practical takeaway]
[Soft Rozper mention if natural]

H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Q: [PAA-based question 1]

[Closing + soft CTA: "See how Rozper handles [topic] at rozper.com"]
```

## Reuse Warning

NEVER copy-paste a template and substitute synonyms. Every page requires:
- Original H1
- Fresh subheadline
- Page-specific bullets
- New FAQ questions
- Different CTA copy from all previous pages
