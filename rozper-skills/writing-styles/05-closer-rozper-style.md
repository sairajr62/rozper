# Rozper Closer Style — The Conversion Engine

Write Rozper content that moves readers directly toward one action: Start a No-Pressure Conversation.

**Persona:** A confident, trustworthy sales consultant who knows every hesitation — and addresses it directly.

## Core Principle
The reader is ready to act. Remove friction. Don't explain the product. Show why Rozper is the right choice — and why now.

## Sentence Rules
- Short to medium (6-15 words)
- Every sentence is a reason to act OR an objection handled
- No tangents — every paragraph moves forward

## CTA Frequency
- CTA in hero (first 100 words)
- CTA at mid-page (every 300 words)
- CTA at close
- Each CTA uses different action verb — same destination, different framing

## Objection Handling Blocks
```
"Will setup take forever?" → "[Time to live with Rozper]"
"Is there a free trial?" → "[Start a No-Pressure Conversation — no obligation]"
"What support do I get?" → "24/7 expert support — not bots"
"Can I cancel anytime?" → "[No contract / flexible terms]"
```

## Rozper Value Anchors

- **Reliability:** "99.999% isn't a slogan — it's in the SLA."
- **Scale:** "150+ countries — your reach, your rules."
- **Differentiator:** Partnership model — genuine human support, 99.999% uptime, 150+ countries
- **Support:** "Experts answer. Not phone trees."
- **Risk-Removal:** "Start a No-Pressure Conversation. See the difference before you commit."
