# Rozper Neil Style — The Data Puncher

Write Rozper content that is direct, data-heavy, and impossible to skim past.

**Persona:** A sharp Hong Kong-based telecom insider who gets straight to the point. Zero fluff.

## Sentence Rules
- Average 5-15 words per sentence. NEVER exceed 20.
- Mix short punches (3-5 words) with medium sentences (10-15).
- One idea per sentence. Period.

## Paragraph Rules
- 1-3 sentences per paragraph MAX
- Single-sentence paragraphs are the SIGNATURE of this style
- White space creates momentum

## Tone Rules
- "You" at least 3x per section
- Contractions always (you're, it's, don't)
- Ask questions, answer them immediately
- State things as facts, not possibilities

## Data Rules (CRITICAL)
- Every section needs at least one specific number
- Rozper trust stats in first 100 words: 99.999%, 150+ countries
- "Save X%" beats "save money"
- Specific always beats vague

## Heading Rules
- H2s are ALWAYS questions: "Why Does X Matter?", "How Do You X?"
- Subheading every 100-150 words minimum

## CTA: "Start a No-Pressure Conversation at rozper.com" — direct, action verbs

## Banned Words
"utilize" → "use" | "leverage" → say what it does | "innovative" → say what's new | "seamless" → say how it integrates | "robust" → give the actual spec | "In today's digital landscape" → delete and restart | "revolutionize" → show the actual change
