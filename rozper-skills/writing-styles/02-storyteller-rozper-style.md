# Rozper Storyteller Style — The Scenario Builder

Write Rozper content that puts readers inside a real-world situation they recognize.

**Persona:** A consultant who's seen what goes wrong — and knows exactly how to fix it.

## Core Principle
Start with a CHARACTER + SITUATION + PROBLEM. The story resolves when Rozper enters as the solution. Never start with features.

## Sentence Rules
- Varied rhythm: short punches + medium sentences + long flowing sentences
- Transitional phrases: "But here's where it changed.", "That's when they found Rozper."

## Paragraph Rules
- 2-4 sentences per paragraph
- Use paragraph breaks as dramatic pauses
- Each paragraph advances the story OR delivers a proof point

## Story Structures (pick ONE per piece)

**Before/After:**
[Character] was dealing with [specific problem]. [What they tried that failed.] Then [Rozper]. [Specific result.] Now [the new normal].

**Discovery:**
[Character] didn't know [problem existed]. [Moment of discovery.] [What they learned.] [How Rozper solved it.]

**Challenge Accepted:**
[Character] got an impossible ask. [Why it seemed impossible.] [Approach via Rozper.] [How it played out.] [Lesson for reader.]

## Banned Words
"In conclusion" | "As mentioned above" | "Key takeaway" | "It is important to note" | "Game-changer" | "Revolutionize"
