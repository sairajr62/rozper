## MISSION & BRAND PHILOSOPHY

Rozper was founded on a specific belief: telecom services feel impersonal and complex — and that needs to change. The brand positions itself not as a vendor but as a partner. The goal isn't to sell a service; it's to understand the client's vision and provide the communications backbone that helps achieve it.

**Brand voice anchor:** "We feel like your own team, not just another provider."

**The three core brand values:**
1. **Reliability first** — 99.999% uptime, engineered into infrastructure
2. **Human support** — 24/7 experts, not bots or phone trees
3. **Genuine partnership** — understand your vision, then solve it

**This is Rozper's strongest differentiator vs Ajoxi, MCM, and MeraTalk:** relationship + partnership positioning, not just specs-and-pricing.

## FULL PRODUCT CATALOG

### 1. UCaaS (Unified Communications)
Integrates voice, video, and messaging into one simple, secure hub.

**Key capabilities:**
- Voice + video + messaging unified in one platform
- Hybrid work enablement — remote and office teams connected
- Productivity-boosting collaboration tools
- Single secure hub — no tool fragmentation
- Scales from startup to enterprise

**Use case:** "Rozper's UCaaS platform was the key to our successful shift to hybrid work." — Customer testimonial

### 2. Virtual Phone Numbers
Local and international numbers in **150+ countries** — largest coverage in the brand family.

**Number types available:**
- Local phone numbers (geographic presence)
- International virtual numbers
- Toll-free numbers
- Business phone numbers
- Second numbers (privacy + personal/work separation)

**Key features:**
- Instant local presence in any market without a physical office
- Call forwarding to any device
- Call recording for compliance and training
- CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho)
- Call monitoring for supervisors
- Number porting with minimal disruption

**Customer result:** "Virtual numbers gave us an instant local presence in new markets, leading directly to a 40% increase in international sales." — Sofia Rossi, Artisan Weavers Collective

### 3. Wholesale Voice
Carrier-grade A-Z VoIP termination for carriers, resellers, and high-volume operations.

**Key capabilities:**
- Premium A-Z voice termination
- CLI (Caller Line Identification) and Non-CLI routes
- SIP trunking
- DID + toll-free numbers
- UCaaS + CPaaS integration
- Built-in redundancy and automated failover
- Real-time scalability with automated resource allocation
- Intelligent load balancing
- Risk-free trial available

**Target buyers:** Carriers, call centers, VoIP resellers, enterprises, ISPs

**Customer testimonial:** "Rozper's wholesale voice network gives us exceptional call quality and the peace of mind we need to serve our own customers flawlessly." — Gunnar Björnsson, Director of Network Operations, Apex Communications

### 4. SIP Trunking
Flexible SIP connectivity for businesses.

**Key capabilities:**
- SIP-based voice over internet
- Compatible with all major SIP phones and PBX systems (customer note: "Can we use existing SIP phones? Yes, absolutely.")
- Cost-effective PSTN connectivity
- Redundant routing

### 5. Bulk SMS
Business messaging at scale.

**Key capabilities:**
- Bulk SMS for marketing and notifications
- SMS API for automated messaging
- WhatsApp Business messaging
- Facebook Messenger integration
- Instagram messaging integration
- Transactional + promotional SMS

### 6. Call Center Solutions
Inbound and outbound contact center capabilities.

**Key capabilities:**
- Advanced call routing and analytics
- CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Zendesk, Freshdesk, ServiceNow)
- Productivity integrations (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack)
- Call monitoring for supervisors
- Call recording
- Real-time performance analytics
- IVR and call-on-hold management
