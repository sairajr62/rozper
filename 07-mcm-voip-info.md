## Platform Features & Capabilities

MCM's voice platform includes advanced capabilities designed for carrier-grade performance and modern telecom operations.

### Multi-Codec Support
- **G.711** (μ-law / A-law) — uncompressed HD voice for premium routes
- **G.729** — bandwidth-efficient codec for high-volume international traffic
- **Opus** — adaptive bitrate codec for variable network conditions
- **AMR-WB** — wideband codec for mobile network interoperability

Codec negotiation happens automatically during call setup, selecting the optimal codec based on route type, destination network, and available bandwidth.

### REST APIs
- **Provisioning API** — programmatic account setup, trunk configuration, and route management
- **Routing API** — dynamic route selection, LCR rule updates, and traffic steering
- **CDR API** — real-time call detail record access for billing, analytics, and reconciliation

Full API documentation enables telecom partners to integrate MCM services directly into their own platforms and billing systems.

### Failover Architecture
- Geo-redundant infrastructure across multiple data center regions
- Automatic failover with sub-2-second switchover time (<2s)
- Active-active routing clusters eliminate single points of failure
- Redundant SIP proxy layers and media relay paths

Traffic reroutes seamlessly during outages without manual intervention, maintaining call continuity for partners and their end users.

### Global Latency Optimization
- Sub-150ms end-to-end latency on major voice corridors
- Distributed Points of Presence (PoPs) across key telecom hubs
- Intelligent routing selects lowest-latency paths per destination
- Continuous latency monitoring with automated path optimization

Low latency ensures natural conversation quality on international calls, critical for call centers and enterprise voice operations.

### WebRTC Support
- Browser-based calling without plugins or softphone installs
- WebRTC-to-SIP gateway for seamless PSTN connectivity
- Enables click-to-call, web-based agent interfaces, and embedded voice apps

Partners can build browser-based communication tools that connect directly to MCM's global voice network.

### QoS & Packet Recovery
- Adaptive jitter buffering for variable network conditions
- Forward Error Correction (FEC) for packet loss recovery
- Real-time QoS monitoring per route and per destination
- Dynamic traffic shaping to prioritize voice packets

These mechanisms maintain voice clarity even on congested or unstable network paths, reducing call quality complaints.

### Geo-Distributed Media Servers
- Media processing clusters deployed across **Americas**, **Europe**, and **APAC**
- Regional media anchoring reduces transcoding hops and latency
- Local media handling keeps RTP streams close to call endpoints

Distributed media architecture improves call quality by minimizing the distance audio packets travel between caller and destination.

## Security & Compliance Framework

### Telecom Compliance Standards

**Section 214 License Compliance**
Operations support regulatory requirements aligned with international telecommunications licensing frameworks, including Section 214 compliance for international voice services.

**Robocall Mitigation Database (RMD) Registration**
The network is registered within the RMD system, allowing compliant voice traffic delivery to the United States telecom ecosystem.

**STIR/SHAKEN Authentication**
Caller authentication technology verifies caller identity and reduces spoofed traffic.

MCM participates in industry traceback initiatives, cooperating with telecom authorities and partners to investigate suspicious traffic activity.
