# 302 Area Code: A Complete Guide to Delaware's Statewide Number

Most states juggle a handful of area codes. Delaware uses exactly one. Dial any landline or cell phone from Wilmington in the north to the beach towns in the south, and the number starts the same way: 302. That rare "one state, one code" status makes the **302 area code** a genuine piece of Delaware identity — and a smart number to own if you do business in the First State.

Here's everything worth knowing about the **302 area code**: where it reaches, the cities it covers, its place in telephone history, the time zone it follows, and how to get a 302 number even if you're nowhere near Delaware.

## The 302 Area Code Covers the Entire First State

There's no boundary line to memorize with the **302 area code**, because it serves all of Delaware. Every one of the state's three counties — New Castle in the north, Kent in the middle, and Sussex along the southern coast — falls under 302. From the corporate offices of Wilmington to the boardwalk of Rehoboth Beach, it's the same three digits.

That makes Delaware one of a small group of U.S. states served by a single area code, alongside places like Rhode Island and Vermont historically. For a state this compact, one code has been enough to go around for decades.

Because the **302 area code** blankets the whole state, it covers every Delaware community, including:

- **New Castle County:** Wilmington (the largest city), Newark, Bear, Middletown, Hockessin, New Castle
- **Kent County:** Dover (the state capital), Smyrna, Milford, Camden, Harrington
- **Sussex County:** Georgetown, Seaford, Lewes, Rehoboth Beach, Millsboro, Laurel, Selbyville

Whether your customer is a financial professional in Wilmington or a vacation-rental owner near the coast, a 302 number reads as local to all of them.

## One of America's Original Area Codes

The **302 area code** isn't just Delaware's only code — it's one of the oldest in the country. It was assigned in 1947 as part of the very first wave of North American Numbering Plan area codes. Many regions have since been split or layered with overlay codes as phone numbers ran short, but Delaware never needed to. Since 1947, **area code 302** has served the state without a single split or overlay across more than 75 years of continuous use.

That longevity is part of why "302" has become shorthand for Delaware itself, showing up on apparel, local brands, and community pride well beyond the phone network. Practically, the single-code setup also means **seven-digit dialing still works** for local calls within the state — a small convenience that overlay regions lost years ago.

## What Time Zone Is the 302 Area Code In?

All of Delaware, and therefore the entire **302 area code**, sits in the Eastern Time Zone. That's Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5) in the colder months and Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4) during daylight saving. If you're scheduling calls or support coverage for a 302 number, treat it as Eastern Time — the same clock as Philadelphia, New York, and Washington, D.C.

## Is a 302 Call Safe? Scams and Spoofing

A common worry: "I got a call from a 302 number I don't recognize — is it a scam?" The honest answer is that the **302 area code** itself is completely legitimate. It's the official, decades-old code for Delaware.

What muddies the water is spoofing. Scammers can fake almost any caller ID, including a local-looking **area code 302** number, to raise the odds you'll answer. So the prefix tells you the call claims to originate in Delaware — nothing more. The safer habit is to judge the specific caller and let unknown numbers go to voicemail, rather than assuming a 302 number is automatically trustworthy or automatically fraudulent.

## Landline, Mobile, or VoIP?

The **302 area code** isn't reserved for any one type of line. It's assigned across residential landlines, mobile phones, businesses, government offices, and modern VoIP services. So a 302 number could be a home phone in Dover, a cell in Newark, or a cloud-based business line routed from another state entirely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is the 302 area code located?**
It covers the entire state of Delaware — all three counties (New Castle, Kent, and Sussex) and every city, from Wilmington and Newark in the north to Dover in the center and Rehoboth Beach in the south.

**What time zone does the 302 area code use?**
This code is entirely in the Eastern Time Zone, observing Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5) in winter and Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4) during daylight saving.

**Is 302 the only area code in Delaware?**
Yes. Delaware has been served by a single area code since 1947. It has never been split or given an overlay, making it one of the few U.S. states covered by just one code.

**Is a call from a 302 number a scam?**
Not inherently. The 302 prefix is legitimate, but scammers can spoof it to look local. Treat the prefix as informational only — evaluate the actual caller and let unfamiliar numbers go to voicemail if you're unsure.

**Can I get a 302 number if I live outside Delaware?**

**Do I need to dial all ten digits for local 302 calls?**
