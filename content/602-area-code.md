## What Is the 602 Area Code?

![Which Parts of Phoenix Use the 602 — Coverage and Overlay Map](/images/blog/602-image-2.webp)

The **602 area code** is a North American Numbering Plan (NANPA)-assigned telephone code covering central Phoenix and the heart of the Maricopa County metro. It was one of the original 86 area codes activated in 1947 — a time when it covered the entire state of Arizona. Decades of explosive population growth forced multiple splits that carved away outlying regions, leaving 602 concentrated in the urban core it has become synonymous with.

## The History of Phoenix's 602: From Statewide to Urban Core

![How the 602 Shaped Phoenix Communication — History Timeline](/images/blog/602-image-1.webp)

When 602 launched in 1947, it was Arizona's only telephone code — one number covered the entire state from Tucson to Flagstaff. Much like how the [213 area code](/blog/213-area-code) once covered all of Los Angeles before being split as the population grew, Arizona's code underwent a series of splits as the Phoenix metro expanded:

- **1995:** Code 520 split off to serve Tucson and southern Arizona
- **1999:** Code 623 split off for the West Valley (Glendale, Peoria, Avondale)
- **1999:** Code 480 split off for the East Valley (Scottsdale, Tempe, Chandler, Mesa)
- **2023:** The Arizona Corporation Commission eliminated geographic boundaries between 480, 602, and 623, creating a unified Phoenix metro overlay — and mandating 10-digit dialing throughout

Similar to how the [415 area code](/blog/415-area-code) remains synonymous with San Francisco despite overlays and splits, the **602 area code** carries 77 years of association with Phoenix's urban core. No other Phoenix prefix holds that history.

## Time Zone and Dialing Rules

![Mountain Standard Time and 10-Digit Dialing Protocol Guide](/images/blog/602-image-3.webp)

The **602 area code** operates in **Mountain Standard Time (MST) — UTC−7, year-round**.

Arizona is one of only a handful of US states that does not observe Daylight Saving Time. When the rest of the country springs forward in March and falls back in November, Phoenix stays put. This creates a unique scheduling advantage for businesses: call windows, CRM automations, and customer notification windows never need biannual reconfiguration.

**Time comparison (summer / winter):**

| Time Zone | Summer offset vs. Phoenix | Winter offset vs. Phoenix |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | +3 hours | +2 hours |
| Central (CT) | +2 hours | +1 hour |
| Mountain (MT) | +1 hour | 0 hours |
| Pacific (PT) | 0 hours | −1 hour |

**Dialing format:**
- Domestic: 1 + 602 + 7-digit number
- International: your country's exit code + 1 + 602 + 7-digit number

10-digit dialing is mandatory throughout the Phoenix metro following the 2023 boundary elimination overlay. If your phone system stores any Phoenix number without the full **area code 602** prefix, update it — 7-digit dialing will not connect.

## Is the 602 Area Code a Scam?

The **602 area code** is legitimate — officially assigned by NANPA to a real geographic region. But Phoenix's growth and visibility make it a frequent spoofing target. The 602 prefix ranks among the top 30 most-complained-about area codes nationally, generating more than 6.5 million bogus robocalls in a single month at peak periods.

**Common 602 scam patterns in 2026:**

- **Utility disconnection fraud (APS/SRP impersonation)** — The most-reported Phoenix scam: callers spoofing local utility company numbers threaten imminent power shutoff unless immediate payment is made. Real utilities send multiple written notices before any disconnection — they never demand same-day phone payment.
- **Law firm impersonation voicemails** — Robocalls claiming to be from law firms warn that a uniformed courier will serve legal documents tied to your Social Security number. These are designed to panic recipients into calling back and providing personal information.
- **Fake package delivery notices** — Door hangers prompt recipients to call a 602 number and provide a confirmation code. The call captures personal data for marketing schemes.

**How to protect yourself:**
1. Let unfamiliar 602 numbers ring to voicemail before calling back
2. Never pay any utility bill via gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency based on a phone call
3. Verify any legal threat by calling the firm directly using a number you find independently
4. Report scam calls to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov or via the FCC complaint portal

STIR/SHAKEN caller authentication — now required by the FCC for US carriers — increasingly marks spoofed 602 calls as "Scam Likely" on compliant networks. Legitimate business numbers from verified providers pass authentication and display correctly.
