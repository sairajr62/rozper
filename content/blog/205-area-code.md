## The History of Birmingham's 205: Alabama's Original Code

**1947:** Area code 205 launched as Alabama's only phone code. It was among the first 86 NANPA codes in the US. Like the [334 area code](/blog/334-area-code), it became tied to its home turf. This happened in the early days of US phone numbers.

**1995:** More people moved to Alabama. Phone numbers ran short. The new 334 area code took over the southeast — Montgomery, Mobile, and Dothan.

**1998:** A second split created 256 for the north and northeast — Huntsville, Decatur, and Gadsden. This left the **205 area code** focused on Birmingham, Tuscaloosa, and central-western Alabama.

**October 2019:** Regulators added the 659 overlay instead of a third split. This kept existing numbers in place. But it also meant every call now needs 10-digit dialing.

## Are 205 Calls Safe?

![Common Alabama Telephone Scams — What to Expect and How to Protect Yourself](/images/blog/205-image-2.webp)

The **205 area code** is real. It is assigned to central and western Alabama. But it is also the most-reported spam area code in the state. Scammers spoof 205 numbers because it gets more people to answer.

Common scams include fake legal threats, Medicare drug spam, and Amazon purchase fraud robocalls. Let strange 205 numbers go to voicemail. Never call back a threatening voicemail. Do not press any digits during a robocall prompt. Report scam calls to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov.
