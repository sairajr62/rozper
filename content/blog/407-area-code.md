## What Is the 407 Area Code?

Area code 407 was created in 1988 when Florida reorganized its area code structure to accommodate the state's explosive growth. Before that, much of Central Florida had been served by 305 (which also covered Miami) and 904 (which covered northern Florida). The reorganization assigned 407 to the Central Florida region anchored by Orlando.

As Orlando continued to grow through the 1990s and 2000s, 407 was further supplemented: area code 321 was added for the Space Coast and surrounding areas in 1999, and area code 689 was added as an overlay for the 407 territory in 2013, meaning ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls.
