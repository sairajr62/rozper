## What Is the 813 Area Code?

![What Is the 813 Area Code?](/images/blog/813-image-1.webp)

This prefix is the primary telephone code for West Central Florida, serving Hillsborough County and portions of Pasco County. Established in 1953, it is one of the Southeast's oldest and most recognized prefixes, having served the Tampa Bay region through decades of population and economic growth.

In 2022, the 656 area code was introduced as an overlay, meaning both codes now serve the same geographic region. Calls placed from either prefix reach the same communities — there is no service or geographic distinction between the two codes.

Hillsborough County forms the core of the coverage zone. The county is home to the Port of Tampa Bay — one of the largest ports in the Southeast by cargo tonnage — MacDill Air Force Base, and the University of South Florida, giving the region a diverse economic base that drives consistent demand for reliable local business communication.

## History of the 813 Area Code

![History of the 813 Area Code](/images/blog/813-image-3.webp)

The **813 area code** launched on January 1, 1953, as a split from Florida's original 305 prefix, which had covered the entire state. At that time, the original **area code 813** territory stretched from Pasco County in the north all the way to Collier County in the southwest, covering a vast stretch of Florida's Gulf Coast.

Population growth in subsequent decades forced two major geographic splits:

- **1995**: The southwestern corridor — including Sarasota, Fort Myers, and Naples — was reassigned to the 941 area code.
- **1998**: Pinellas County (St. Petersburg and Clearwater) was separated and given the 727 area code.

These divisions reduced the 813 footprint to its current Hillsborough-centered territory. When the region next faced number exhaustion, regulators chose an overlay rather than another geographic split: the 656 area code was activated on February 22, 2022, adding a new pool of numbers without displacing any existing subscribers.

## 813 Area Code Time Zone and the 656 Overlay

![813 Area Code Time Zone and the 656 Overlay](/images/blog/813-image-4.webp)

All phone numbers in this region — whether carrying the **813 area code** prefix or the newer 656 overlay — operate on **Eastern Time (ET)**. That means Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC-5) during winter months and Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4) from spring through autumn.

Since the 656 overlay launched in 2022, mandatory 10-digit dialing applies to all calls within the service area. Every local call must include the full area code before the seven-digit number — seven-digit dialing no longer connects, regardless of whether you are calling an 813 or 656 number.

For businesses scheduling calls with Tampa-area contacts from other time zones, a 9:00 a.m. Tampa meeting is 6:00 a.m. Pacific and 2:00 p.m. in London. Knowing the dialing protocol and time zone for **area code 813** prevents scheduling errors and missed connections for distributed teams operating across regions.
