## What Is the 813 Area Code?

![What Is the 813 Area Code?](/images/blog/813-image-1.webp)

This prefix is the main phone code for West Central Florida. It serves Hillsborough County and parts of Pasco County. It started in 1953. That makes it one of the Southeast's oldest and best-known codes. It has served the Tampa Bay region through decades of growth.

In 2022, the 656 area code joined as an overlay. Both codes now serve the same region. A call from either prefix reaches the same communities. There is no service gap or location difference between the two codes.

Hillsborough County sits at the heart of the coverage zone. The county holds the Port of Tampa Bay, one of the largest ports in the Southeast by cargo weight. It is also home to MacDill Air Force Base and the University of South Florida. Together, these anchors give the region a broad economic base. That base drives steady demand for solid local business calls.

## History of the 813 Area Code

![History of the 813 Area Code](/images/blog/813-image-3.webp)

The **813 area code** launched on January 1, 1953. It split off from Florida's original 305 prefix, which once covered the whole state. Back then, the **area code 813** territory ran from Pasco County in the north. It reached all the way to Collier County in the southwest. That covered a huge stretch of Florida's Gulf Coast.

Growth in later decades forced two big splits:

- **1995**: The southwestern corridor, including Sarasota, Fort Myers, and Naples, moved to the 941 area code.
- **1998**: Pinellas County (St. Petersburg and Clearwater) split off and got the 727 area code.

These two splits shrank the 813 footprint down to today's Hillsborough-centered area. When the region ran low on numbers again, regulators chose a different fix. Instead of another split, they added an overlay. The 656 area code went live on February 22, 2022. It added a fresh pool of numbers without changing any existing subscriber's number.

## 813 Area Code Time Zone and the 656 Overlay

![813 Area Code Time Zone and the 656 Overlay](/images/blog/813-image-4.webp)

Every phone number in this region runs on **Eastern Time (ET)**. This is true for both the **813 area code** and the newer 656 overlay. That means Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC-5) in winter and Eastern Daylight Time (EDT, UTC-4) from spring through fall.

Since the 656 overlay launched in 2022, you must dial all 10 digits for every call in the service area. Each local call needs the full area code before the seven-digit number. Seven-digit dialing no longer works, whether you call an 813 or 656 number.

Do you schedule calls with Tampa contacts from other time zones? Keep this in mind: a 9:00 a.m. Tampa meeting is 6:00 a.m. on the Pacific coast and 2:00 p.m. in London. Knowing the dial rule and time zone for **area code 813** helps you avoid scheduling errors. It also helps distributed teams stay connected across regions.
