## What Is the 917 Area Code and Where Does It Apply?

The 917 area code is a citywide overlay for New York City. Unlike area codes that serve a single county or metro district, 917 covers all five boroughs simultaneously — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, The Bronx, and Staten Island — alongside the other NYC overlay codes (212, 332, 646, 718, 347, and 929).

When 917 launched in 1992, it was assigned exclusively to cellular phones and pagers, making it the first US code primarily tied to mobile communications. That early mobile identity gives 917 numbers a particular resonance with business contacts — they read as active, reachable, and metropolitan.

**Time zone:** Eastern Time (ET) — UTC−5 (November to March) and UTC−4 during Daylight Saving Time.

Key milestones: 917 introduced in 1992 as the first NANP overlay; extended to landlines in 2011; area code 465 approved in 2026 as existing codes approach exhaustion. If establishing a 917 number is on your roadmap, now is the time.
