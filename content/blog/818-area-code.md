## What Is the 818 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 818 area code entered service on January 7, 1984, split from the original 213. On June 14, 1997, the eastern San Gabriel Valley was carved off into 626, tightening 818's focus on the San Fernando Valley. By the 2000s, numbers were thinning; regulators added the 747 overlay in May 2009, covering the same geography without displacing any existing 818 subscriber. For businesses today, an established 818 number signals a presence that predates the crowd — a meaningful advantage in one of the most call-screened markets in the country.

**What is the 747 area code, and how does it relate to 818?**
747 is an overlay code introduced May 18, 2009 that shares the same geographic area as 818; new numbers in the region may carry either prefix, but both serve the same San Fernando Valley territory.

**Do I need to dial ten digits for 818 numbers?**
Yes — because 818 and 747 overlay the same territory, ten-digit dialing is required for all calls, including local ones.
