## What Is the 818 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 818 area code is woven into the story of how Los Angeles outgrew a single code. It entered service on January 7, 1984, split from the original 213 — one of the first times a major U.S. city was carved into multiple numbering areas. At its creation, 818 served a broad stretch of the greater LA region. As the Valley and its neighbors kept growing, the code was refined: on June 14, 1997, the eastern San Gabriel Valley was spun off into the new 626 area code, sharpening 818's identity around the San Fernando Valley.

By the 2000s, available 818 numbers were thinning out. Rather than split the region again — which would have forced existing holders to change their numbers — regulators approved an overlay: the 747 area code, which took effect on May 18, 2009. Because 747 overlays the same geography, it shares 818's exact boundaries and simply adds a fresh pool of numbers. Every existing 818 subscriber kept their number untouched.

The practical result for businesses today: an established 818 number now signals a presence that predates the crowd — a subtle but meaningful marker of being here first, in one of the most media-saturated and call-screened markets in the country.

**What is the 747 area code, and how is it related to 818?**
747 is an overlay code introduced on May 18, 2009 that shares the exact same geographic area as 818. It was added to create more available numbers without changing anyone's existing line or splitting the region geographically. New numbers in the region may be issued with either an 818 or 747 code — both serve the same San Fernando Valley territory.

Yes. Because 818 and 747 overlay the same territory, ten-digit dialing is required for all calls — including local ones. Always dial the full 818 area code followed by the seven-digit number, even when calling next door.

**What time zone is the 818 area code in?**
The 818 area code is entirely in the Pacific Time Zone — PST (UTC-8) in standard time and PDT (UTC-7) during daylight saving. Schedule any calls to the region around Pacific business hours.
