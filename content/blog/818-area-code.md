## What Is the 818 Area Code?

Area code 818 was established on January 7, 1984, as a split from the original 213 area code that had served all of Los Angeles County since the North American Numbering Plan's 1947 launch. As Los Angeles County's population and commercial demand surged through the late 1970s and early 1980s, 213 alone could no longer supply the number of telephone numbers the region needed.

The 1984 reorganization separated the San Fernando Valley from the urban LA core, assigning 818 to the Valley while retaining 213 for the downtown Los Angeles and inner-ring communities. In 1997, area code 747 was introduced as an overlay for the same territory as 818, meaning both codes now cover the San Fernando Valley simultaneously. All local calls within the 818/747 overlay require 10-digit dialing.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** The northern portion of Los Angeles County — the San Fernando Valley and adjacent communities including Pasadena and Glendale.
