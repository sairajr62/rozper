## The History of San Antonio's 210: From 512 to Its Own Identity

When the original US area codes were established in 1947, all of central and south Texas shared the 512 area code. The mobile phone explosion of the late 1980s changed that.

**1992:** The Texas Public Utility Commission approved a geographic split, assigning 210 to San Antonio and south-central Texas. Austin retained 512, giving San Antonio its own telephonic identity. Regulators later introduced the 726 overlay to handle number demand without another split. The **210 area code** now carries 30+ years of San Antonio identity — the code businesses reach for when local recognition matters most. Just as [the 281 area code](/blog/281-area-code) shaped Houston's identity, 210 anchors south-central Texas.

## Understanding the 210 and 726 Overlay

The 726 area code is a full overlay for the same south-central Texas region. Both codes serve every address in the same counties, and 10-digit dialing is mandatory. Despite identical coverage, area code 210 carries 30+ years of San Antonio brand recognition — a clear advantage for businesses seeking established local credibility.

## Time Zone and Dialing Rules

The **210 area code** operates in **Central Time — CST (UTC−6) in winter, CDT (UTC−5) in summer**. San Antonio observes Daylight Saving Time — one hour behind Eastern and one hour ahead of Mountain. Dial domestically as 1 + 210 + 7-digit number. Ten-digit dialing is mandatory — 7-digit dialing will not connect.
