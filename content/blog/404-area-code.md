# 404 Area Code: Atlanta Location, Time Zone & Business Numbers

The **404 area code** is Atlanta's first phone prefix. It was one of 86 codes set up in 1947 under the North American Numbering Plan. It is still the city's best-known dialing code today. Atlanta grew fast through the 1990s and 2000s. To handle that growth, codes 678, 770, and 470 were added to the wider metro. But 404 stayed tied to the main city core. That core is Fulton, DeKalb, and Clayton counties. It covers Atlanta, Sandy Springs, Decatur, East Point, and College Park. All four codes share one rule: you must dial all 10 digits. Numbers run on **Eastern Time (ET)**. That means EST (UTC-5) in winter and EDT (UTC-4) in summer. This puts Atlanta in step with New York and other East Coast business hubs. That match helps firms that run sales or support teams up and down the coast. A 404 number keeps work hours in sync with other East Coast markets. There is no need to shift time zones for every call.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the 404 area code?**
The 404 area code is Atlanta's original telephone prefix. It serves the city and nearby communities in Fulton, DeKalb, and Clayton counties. It was set up in 1947 and remains Atlanta's most recognized dialing identity.

**What cities does the 404 serve?**
The main service area covers Atlanta, Sandy Springs, Decatur, East Point, and College Park. Nearby suburbs and metro communities are served by overlay codes 678, 770, and 470. These share the same geographic footprint.

**What time zone is Atlanta's 404 in?**
All 404 numbers run on Eastern Time. That is EST (UTC-5) in fall and winter, and EDT (UTC-4) in spring and summer. Atlanta lines up with New York and other East Coast business hubs.

**Do I need a physical Atlanta address to get a 404 number?**
No. Virtual phone providers let any business get a 404 number and route calls to any device, from any location. No Georgia address or physical office is required.

**Is 10-digit dialing required for the 404 area code?**
Yes. Because 404 shares the Atlanta metro with overlay codes 678, 770, and 470, all local calls need the full 10-digit format. Seven-digit dialing will not connect in the region.

**Can I transfer my existing 404 number to a new provider?**
Yes. Local number portability lets you transfer any eligible 404 number without changing your digits. The process usually takes hours to a few business days. You will need a Letter of Authorization and a recent phone bill.
