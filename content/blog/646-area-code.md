## What Is the 646 Area Code?

The **646 area code** is a telephone area code in the North American Numbering Plan (NANP) assigned exclusively to the borough of Manhattan, New York City. It operates as an overlay code, sharing Manhattan's geographic territory with the original 212 code rather than splitting it geographically. Introduced in 1999, **area code 646** expanded the pool of available Manhattan numbers as demand from landlines, mobile phones, and business lines outpaced the 212 supply.

Today, Manhattan runs four active codes simultaneously — 212, 646, 332, and 917. All four are legitimate Manhattan numbers requiring mandatory 10-digit dialing. The **646 area code** is among the most widely recognized New York City codes alongside its overlay partners.

## A Brief Timeline of Manhattan Area Codes

The **646 area code** was born out of necessity. Manhattan's telecommunications infrastructure faced overwhelming demand through the 1990s as fax machines, early cellular networks, corporate lines, and internet-era businesses drove demand far beyond what the 212 code alone could handle.

Key Manhattan area code milestones:

- **1947:** Area code 212 assigned to all of New York State under the NANP
- **1984:** Outer boroughs split off to 718; 212 became Manhattan-only
- **1992:** Area code 917 introduced as the first mobile and pager overlay for all five boroughs
- **1999:** The **646 area code** launched as a general overlay, immediately requiring mandatory 10-digit dialing across Manhattan
- **2017:** Area code 332 activated as a fourth relief overlay for new Manhattan assignments

The introduction of **area code 646** marked a turning point in how New York City managed number capacity. ## Time Zone, Format, and 10-Digit Dialing Requirements

All **646 area code** numbers operate in the **Eastern Time Zone (ET)**:

- **Eastern Standard Time (EST):** UTC−5, from early November through mid-March
- **Eastern Daylight Time (EDT):** UTC−4, from mid-March through early November

For businesses in Pacific Time calling Manhattan during standard business hours (9 AM–6 PM ET), outreach windows fall between 6 AM and 3 PM PT.

Mandatory 10-digit dialing is a fixed requirement for the **646 area code** due to Manhattan's overlay structure. Because four codes share the same territory, every call — local or long-distance — must use the complete format: 1 + 646 + 7-digit local number. Dialing only 7 digits results in a failed connection. Authoritative guidance on overlay dialing requirements is published by the [FCC's consumer ten-digit dialing guide](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/ten-digit-dialing), the official US government reference for these regulations.
