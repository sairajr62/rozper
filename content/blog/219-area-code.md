## A short history of the 219 area code

The **219 area code** dates back to 1947, when it was assigned to the entire northern half of Indiana as one of the state's original three area codes, alongside 317 for central Indiana and 812 for the south. For decades, 219 covered a huge territory that stretched from Gary all the way to Fort Wayne and South Bend.

As Indiana's population and phone demand grew, that territory eventually became too large for one code to sustain. In 2002, the original 219 footprint was split. The 574 area code took over north-central Indiana, including South Bend and Elkhart, while the 260 area code took over the northeast, including Fort Wayne. What remained under **area code 219** was the compact Northwest Indiana region tied to the Chicago metro. Numbering-plan changes like these follow the area-code relief and dialing procedures the [FCC documents for consumers nationwide](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/ten-digit-dialing). More than two decades after that split, 219 is still the clearest shorthand for Northwest Indiana.
