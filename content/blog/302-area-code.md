# 302 Area Code: A Complete Guide to Delaware's Statewide Number

Most states juggle a handful of area codes. Delaware uses just one. That rare "one state, one code" status makes the **302 area code** a real piece of Delaware identity. It's also a smart number to own if you do business in the First State. This guide covers what you need to know. We'll look at where the code reaches, the cities it serves, its phone history, the time zone it follows, and how to get a 302 number from anywhere.

## The 302 Area Code Covers the Entire First State

There's no boundary line to memorize with the **302 area code**. It serves all of Delaware. All three counties fall under 302: New Castle in the north, Kent in the middle, and Sussex along the southern coast. From the office towers of Wilmington to the boardwalk of Rehoboth Beach, it's the same three digits.

That makes Delaware one of a small group of U.S. states served by a single area code. For a state this compact, one code has been enough for decades. There are no plans to change that anytime soon.

## One of America's Original Area Codes

The **302 area code** isn't just Delaware's only code. It's one of the oldest in the country. It was set in 1947, part of the very first wave of North American area codes. Many regions have since been split or layered with extra overlay codes as phone numbers ran short. Delaware never needed to make that change.

Since 1947, **area code 302** has served the state without a single split or overlay. That's over 75 years of continuous use. This long run is part of why "302" has become shorthand for Delaware itself. You'll see it on apparel, local brands, and signs of community pride. The single-code setup also means **seven-digit dialing still works** for local calls in the state. That's a small perk that overlay regions lost years ago. For solid state facts on Delaware business licenses and county details, the [Delaware official government site](https://delaware.gov/) is a good spot for new businesses to check.

## What Time Zone Does the 302 Use?

All of Delaware, and so the whole **302 area code**, sits in the Eastern Time Zone. That's Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5) in the colder months and Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4) during daylight saving. If you're scheduling calls or support coverage for a 302 number, treat it as Eastern Time. It's the same clock as Philadelphia, New York, and Washington, D.C.

Knowing the time zone matters most when your team is spread out. A sales rep in California calling a Delaware prospect should plan for a three-hour gap during standard hours.

## Is a 302 Call Safe? Scams and Spoofing

A common worry: "I got a call from a 302 number I don't recognize. Is it a scam?" The honest answer is that the **302 area code** itself is fully legit. It's the official, decades-old code for Delaware. What muddies the water is spoofing. That's when scammers fake a local-looking **area code 302** number to raise the odds you'll answer.

The prefix only tells you the call claims to come from Delaware. Nothing more. The safer habit is to judge the specific caller and let unknown numbers go to voicemail. If you get repeated unwanted calls from a 302 number, you can report it to the Federal Trade Commission and to your phone carrier's fraud team.

Delaware's simple single-code system has kept things easy for residents and businesses for nearly eight decades. No ten-digit dialing needed for local calls.
