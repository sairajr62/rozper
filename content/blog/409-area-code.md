## Where Does the 409 Area Code Cover?

![Map of the Southeast Texas prefix region](/images/blog/409-image-1.png)

The 409 area code covers the Golden Triangle cities of Beaumont, Port Arthur, and Orange. It also reaches south to Galveston Island. Together, these towns form a mix of refineries, ports, and coastal life along the upper Gulf Coast.

Key cities in the 409 footprint include:

- **Beaumont.** The largest city in the region and a hub for energy and healthcare jobs.
- **Port Arthur.** Home to one of the biggest refining complexes in the country.
- **Galveston.** A historic port city that draws tourists from across Texas.
- **Orange.** An industrial town near the Louisiana border.
- **Texas City.** A key site for petrochemical plants on Galveston Bay.
- **Nederland, Lumberton, and Groves.** Smaller towns that round out the Golden Triangle.

Jefferson County anchors this economy. It hosts some of the largest oil and chemical plants in the nation. That industrial base keeps demand for local phone lines steady. ## How the 409 Area Code Came to Be

![Timeline of the Southeast Texas numbering split](/images/blog/409-image-2.png)

The 409 area code went live on March 19, 1983. It split off from Houston's original 713 prefix. At first, the new territory formed a wide horseshoe. It wrapped around Houston from Galveston up to Nacogdoches and over to Bryan-College Station.

That shape did not last. Texas kept growing fast. In the year 2000, regulators split the old 409 zone three ways:

- The **eastern part**, with Beaumont, Port Arthur, and Galveston, kept the 409 area code.
- The **central part**, near Nacogdoches, moved to the [936 area code](/blog/936-area-code).
- The **western part**, near Bryan-College Station, became area code 979.

That split is why today's 409 area is smaller than it once was. It is also why the code has never needed an overlay since.

## 409 Area Code Time Zone and Dialing

![Central Time zone coverage for Southeast Texas](/images/blog/409-image-3.png)

Every number in the 409 area code runs on **Central Time**. That means CST (UTC−6) in winter and CDT (UTC−5) in summer. Beaumont sits two hours ahead of the West Coast. It sits one hour behind the East Coast.

Because no overlay exists here, seven-digit dialing still works for local calls placed within the same prefix. That sets this code apart from newer overlay regions, where every call needs all ten digits. Teams calling in from outside the area should still dial the full ten digits. This helps avoid routing errors.

For planning calls, a 10 a.m. meeting in Beaumont lines up with 8 a.m. on the West Coast. It lines up with 4 p.m. in London. Checking the time zone first helps remote teams avoid missed calls with Gulf Coast partners.

Daylight saving time shifts twice a year, in March and November. Teams that work across several states should mark these dates on a shared calendar. This small step avoids a lot of confusion when scheduling calls with Beaumont offices.

## Is a 409 Area Code Call a Scam?

Most calls from the 409 area code are real. They come from local businesses, refineries, and Gulf Coast residents going about their day. Still, scammers do target well-known prefixes like this one. Common scams include fake utility shut-off threats and bogus prize offers tied to local events.

Real callers from Southeast Texas will never ask for gift cards or wire transfers on the spot. If a call feels off, hang up. Then call the business back using a number from its own website.

Spam-blocking apps can help filter out robocalls before they ring your phone at all. Businesses that run a real 409 line should also use a VoIP provider with STIR/SHAKEN caller ID checks. This keeps their calls from looking like spam to the people they are trying to reach.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is the 409 area code located?**
It covers Southeast Texas, including Beaumont, Port Arthur, Orange, and Galveston, across five counties along the upper Gulf Coast.

**What cities does the 409 area code cover?**
Coverage includes Beaumont, Port Arthur, Galveston, Orange, Texas City, Nederland, Lumberton, and Groves.

**What time zone does the 409 area code use?**
Numbers here run on Central Time. That is CST (UTC−6) in winter and CDT (UTC−5) in summer.

**Does the 409 area code have an overlay?**
No. This region has never needed one and still allows seven-digit local dialing.

**Why did the 409 area code shrink from its original size?**
A three-way split in 2000 moved the Nacogdoches area to 936 and the Bryan-College Station area to 979. That left the smaller footprint used today.

**Can I get a Beaumont number if my business is based elsewhere?**
Yes. Virtual phone providers can issue a 409 number to any business and route calls to any device, with no Texas address required.

**Is seven-digit dialing still allowed here?**
Yes, for calls placed within the same local prefix. Calls to or from other area codes still need all ten digits.
