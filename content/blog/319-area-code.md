## What Is the 319 Area Code?

![What Is the 319 Area Code](/images/blog/What Is the 319 Area Code.webp)

The **319 area code** was introduced in 1981 as part of a split from the original 515 code, which previously served all of Iowa as a single-code territory. As the state's population and business activity grew, a separate code was needed for the eastern and central portion — resulting in the creation of 319.

Today, the **319 area code** operates as a standalone code covering 12 counties across eastern and central Iowa. Unlike many major metro regions that now operate under multi-code overlay systems, 319 currently has no active overlay partner. Every new number assigned in this territory still carries the 319 prefix, which means the code retains strong local identity without the confusion that overlays can introduce.

The region operates on **Central Time (CT)** — UTC-6 during standard time (early November through mid-March) and UTC-5 during daylight saving time (mid-March through early November). Businesses in Eastern or Pacific time zones should account for the time difference when scheduling outreach to Cedar Rapids or Iowa City contacts.

## History of Area Code 319

![History of Area Code 319](/images/blog/History of Area Code 319.webp)

**Area code 319** was created in 1981 when Iowa's growth made it necessary to divide the state's original 515 code into separate regions. Eastern and central Iowa were assigned 319, while 515 retained central and western Iowa including Des Moines.

Key milestones that shaped the 319 territory:

- **1981:** The 319 code was introduced, serving eastern and central Iowa across 12 counties
- **2000:** Area code 563 was split from 319 to serve northeastern Iowa, including Dubuque and the Quad Cities corridor
- **Present:** The **319 area code** continues as a standalone code serving its core territory — Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, Waterloo, and surrounding counties

When the **319 area code** was first established, Cedar Rapids was already Iowa's second-largest city. Today the Cedar Rapids metropolitan area anchors a diverse economy in advanced manufacturing, financial technology, and agribusiness services — making local phone presence in this market a practical advantage for any business entering the region. ## FAQ'S

**What cities does the 319 code serve?**
This code covers 12 counties across eastern and central Iowa, including Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, Waterloo, Coralville, Marion, and Vinton. Following the 2000 split that created area code 563, northeastern Iowa — including Dubuque and the Quad Cities corridor — was moved to the 563 code, leaving 319 to serve the core eastern and central Iowa region.

**Does the 319 code have an overlay?**
No. This code currently operates as a standalone code with no active overlay partner. Unlike metro regions that use multiple overlapping codes to meet number demand, 319 continues to assign all new numbers within the same prefix — preserving strong local recognition without the dialing complexity that overlay systems can introduce.

**Do I need an Iowa address to get a 319 number?**
No. VoIP providers allow businesses anywhere to obtain and operate a local Iowa number without a physical Iowa address or office. Calls route to any device your team uses — mobile, desktop, or desk phone — regardless of where your staff is actually located.

**What time zone is the 319 code in?**
The 319 code operates in Central Time (CT): UTC-6 during standard time and UTC-5 during daylight saving time. For businesses in other time zones, plan Iowa outreach calls between approximately 8 AM and 6 PM CT to reach Cedar Rapids and Iowa City contacts during their standard working day.

**What is the difference between 319 and 563?**
The 319 and 563 codes both serve Iowa but cover different geographic regions. **Area code 319** covers eastern and central Iowa — Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, and Waterloo — while 563 covers northeastern Iowa including Dubuque, Davenport, and the Quad Cities area along the Mississippi River. Both codes are recognized as local within their respective Iowa territories.
