## What Is the 408 Area Code?

Area code 408 was created in 1984, when it split from area code 415 as demand for numbers in the South Bay grew with the region's booming technology sector. Before the split, 415 served the entire Bay Area. As Silicon Valley emerged as the center of the global technology industry, the demand for new numbers in the South Bay quickly outpaced what 415 alone could supply.

Area code 669 was added as an overlay for the same territory in 2012, meaning both 408 and 669 serve the same geographic area and ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls.
