## What Is the 412 Area Code?

Area code 412 is one of the original area codes from 1947, assigned to southwestern Pennsylvania when the North American Numbering Plan first launched. For decades, 412 served a large portion of western Pennsylvania. As the region grew and evolved, the code was progressively refined: area code 724 was split off in 1998 to serve the surrounding suburbs and western Pennsylvania outside of the Pittsburgh core.

Today, 412 serves the city of Pittsburgh and its immediate surrounding communities in Allegheny County. Area code 878 was added as an overlay in 2001, meaning ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls.
