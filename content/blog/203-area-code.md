# 203 Area Code: Southwestern Connecticut — Cities, History, Scams & Business Guide

Operating in southwestern Connecticut without a local phone number means losing deals to competitors who look like they belong. The **203 area code** closes that gap — this guide covers cities, history, scam alerts, and how to get a 203 number for your business.

## What Is the 203 Area Code?

The **203 area code** is a NANPA-assigned telephone code covering southwestern Connecticut — Fairfield County and New Haven County. Established in 1947 as one of the original 86 North American area codes, it served the entire state before population growth drove a 1995 geographic split and a 2009 overlay.

## The History of Connecticut's 203: From Statewide Code to Regional Identity

**1947:** Area code 203 launched as the state's only telephone code, covering the entire state from Greenwich to Storrs.

**1995:** Central and eastern regions — Hartford, New Britain, Norwich — were reassigned to the new 860 area code, concentrating the 203 in Fairfield and New Haven counties. Just as [the 201 area code](/blog/201-area-code) serves northern New Jersey with a long legacy, the 203 remains the foundational identifier for southwestern Connecticut.

**2009:** As inventory ran low, regulators launched the 475 overlay on December 12, 2009, covering the identical geographic footprint. The **203 area code** now shares its territory with 475 but carries more than 75 years of brand recognition.

## Understanding the 203 and 475 Overlay

**Same geography, different vintage.** Both the **203 area code** and 475 serve exactly the same towns and cities — a business in Greenwich may hold either or both.

**New lines, either code.** When a new number is provisioned in southwestern Connecticut, the carrier assigns it under 203 or 475 depending on inventory.

**Mandatory 10-digit dialing.** Because both codes share the same geography, all 10 digits are required for every call.

**Brand recognition gap.** Despite identical coverage, 203 carries significantly more brand recognition; 475 is functionally equivalent but reads as newer.

## Time Zone and Dialing Rules

![How to Read a Connecticut Phone Number — Dialing Format Guide](/images/blog/203-image-2.webp)

The **203 area code** operates in **Eastern Time (ET) — EST (UTC−5) in winter, EDT (UTC−4) in summer**. Connecticut observes Daylight Saving Time, with clocks moving forward in mid-March and back in early November.

| Time Zone | Offset vs. Connecticut (ET) |
|---|---|
| Central (CT) | −1 hour |
| Mountain (MT) | −2 hours |
| Pacific (PT) | −3 hours |
| UK (GMT/BST) | +5 hours (EST) / +4 hours (EDT) |

Domestic calls to area code 203 use 1 + 203 + 7-digit number; international callers use their exit code + 1 + 203 + 7 digits. 10-digit dialing is mandatory throughout the overlay region.

## Is the 203 Area Code a Scam?

The **203 area code** is legitimate — officially assigned to southwestern Connecticut. Like any major metro code, it is exploited by scammers who spoof local numbers to increase answer rates.

- **Utility shutoff impersonation** — Callers impersonate Eversource or United Illuminating and demand gift card or wire payment; these utilities never demand same-day phone payments.
- **Legal threat fraud** — Callers use fake legal language claiming the recipient can settle a lawsuit over the phone.
- **Neighbor spoofing** — Scammers match the first six digits of a local area code 203 number to appear local.

Let unfamiliar 203 numbers ring to voicemail and verify utility threats by calling the number on your bill. Never pay alleged debts by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What cities are covered by the 203 area code?**

The **203 area code** covers Fairfield County and New Haven County. Major cities include Bridgeport, New Haven, Stamford, Norwalk, Danbury, Greenwich, Waterbury, and Stratford, among more than 50 towns.

**What is the 475 and how does it relate to 203?**

The 475 is a full overlay introduced December 12, 2009, covering the identical southwestern Connecticut footprint as 203. Both codes serve the same geography, but 203 carries more brand recognition because it predates the overlay by more than 60 years. Businesses that want the strongest local identity typically request a 203 number specifically.

**Is 203 used by scammers?**

Area code 203 is legitimate; scammers spoof it to appear local. The most common scheme is utility shutoff fraud impersonating Eversource or United Illuminating — hang up and call your utility directly using the number on your bill.

**Can I get a 203 number if my business is based outside Connecticut?**

Yes. Virtual phone providers allow businesses anywhere to provision a 203 number, and FCC portability rules let you keep it if you switch providers.
