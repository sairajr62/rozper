## What Is the 616 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 616 area code carries more history than most. It was one of the original North American area codes established in 1947, when the first nationwide telephone numbering plan divided Michigan into three regions. At launch, 616 covered the entire western half of the Lower Peninsula plus all of the Upper Peninsula — making it the largest numbering plan area east of the Mississippi River that didn't serve an entire state.

Over the following decades, population growth and the explosion of cell phones and pagers repeatedly forced the region to split off new area codes. In 1961, the Upper Peninsula was carved out as area code 906. In 1999, the northwestern Lower Peninsula — including Muskegon, Traverse City, and Cadillac — became area code 231. Then in 2002, the lower third of the territory, including Kalamazoo and Battle Creek, was reassigned to area code 269. What remained is today's 616: a tightly defined footprint centered squarely on the Grand Rapids metro and the West Michigan Lakeshore.

Since October 2021, the 616 area code has required ten-digit dialing for all local calls. This transition was implemented nationwide to accommodate 988 as a three-digit access code for the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline.

### Geographic Coverage

The 616 area code serves all of Kent County and Ottawa County, along with portions of Allegan, Barry, Ionia, and Montcalm counties — covering one of Michigan's most economically productive and fastest-growing regions.
