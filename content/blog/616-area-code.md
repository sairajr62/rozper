## What Is the 616 Area Code?

Area code 616 was one of the original North American Numbering Plan codes established in 1947. At that time, it served all of western Michigan as a single code. As the region's population and commercial density grew, 616's territory was split multiple times. In 1993, the northern portions were separated into area code 231. Today, 616 covers Kent County (Grand Rapids), Ottawa County (Holland, Zeeland), Allegan County, and surrounding communities in the southwestern corner of Michigan.

**Time zone:** Eastern Time (ET) — UTC−5 standard, UTC−4 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** West Michigan — Kent County (Grand Rapids, Wyoming, Kentwood, Grandville, Walker), Ottawa County (Holland, Zeeland, Grand Haven, Hudsonville), Allegan County, Barry County, and adjacent communities.
