## 616 History, Coverage, and Cities

The 616 area code is one of the first North American area codes. It was set up in 1947, when the first US-wide phone number plan split Michigan into three parts. Over time, more and more people moved in. This growth forced the area to split off new area codes, again and again. One case is the [231 area code](/blog/231-area-code), made in 1999 for the northwest part of the Lower Peninsula. What is left today is 616. It covers a clear, tight zone centered on Grand Rapids and the West Michigan Lakeshore.

Since October 2021, all local calls in the 616 area code need ten-digit dialing. This change made room for 988, the new three-digit number for the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline.

### Geographic Coverage

The 616 area code covers all of Kent County and Ottawa County. It also covers parts of Allegan, Barry, Ionia, and Montcalm counties.

**Major Cities and Communities in the 616 Area Code:**

- **Grand Rapids** — The second-largest city in Michigan; a hub for health care, factories, and tech
- **Wyoming and Kentwood** — Major trade hubs that anchor the Grand Rapids metro
- **Holland and Grand Haven** — Lakeshore towns with strong factories and small firms
- **Walker, Grandville, Greenville** — Key trade areas in the wider metro area
- **Rockford, Caledonia, Ada** — Growing towns in the outer Grand Rapids metro
