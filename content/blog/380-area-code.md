## What Is the 380 Area Code?

Area code 380 was established in 2011 as an overlay for the Columbus, Ohio metropolitan area. The original area code for Columbus is 614, which has served the city and surrounding counties since 1947. As the Columbus metro grew rapidly, the demand for new phone numbers outpaced what 614 alone could supply. Rather than splitting the geographic territory, 380 was added as an overlay — meaning both 380 and 614 serve exactly the same area, and ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls.

Both 380 and 614 are recognized as Columbus local numbers by anyone in the market.
