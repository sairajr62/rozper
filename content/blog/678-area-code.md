## What Is the 678 Area Code?

Area code 678 was introduced in 1998 as an overlay for area code 770, which had been split from the original 404 in 1995. As Atlanta's explosive growth in the 1990s exhausted the 770 number pool, 678 was added to provide additional capacity for the same geographic territory — the suburbs and outer ring of the Atlanta metro.

**Time zone:** Eastern Time (ET) — UTC−5 standard, UTC−4 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** The Atlanta metropolitan area outside the core 404 territory — including Fulton County suburbs, Gwinnett County, Cobb County, DeKalb County, Cherokee County, Forsyth County, Henry County, Clayton County, Douglas County, and dozens of other suburban communities across the 20+ county Atlanta metro.

**Major cities served:** Alpharetta, Roswell, Marietta, Sandy Springs, Dunwoody, Smyrna, Kennesaw, Lawrenceville, Duluth, Peachtree City, Johns Creek, and numerous other suburban Atlanta communities.
