## What Is the 805 Area Code?

Area code 805 has served the California Central Coast since 1984, when the North American Numbering Plan reorganized California's telephone numbering to accommodate the state's explosive population and economic growth. At that time, much of California was still served by a small number of codes that had been established in 1947. The 1984 reorganization created distinct regional codes for different parts of the state, with 805 assigned to the Central Coast corridor.

The Central Coast's growth, while significant, has been moderate enough that 805 has remained the single defining code for the region — one of the more stable area codes in California.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** Ventura County, Santa Barbara County, and San Luis Obispo County — a coastal corridor spanning roughly 200 miles along the Pacific coast, from the Greater Los Angeles border in the south to just below the Monterey Bay Area in the north.

The 805 area code covers three distinct counties, each with its own economic character and business community.

**San Luis Obispo County:** San Luis Obispo, Paso Robles, Atascadero, Arroyo Grande, Pismo Beach. Key industries: Cal Poly San Luis Obispo's technology and engineering ecosystem, wine production (Paso Robles AVA), agriculture, tourism, and a growing remote-worker economy attracted by the region's quality of life.
