## What Is the 954 Area Code?

![Map highlighting Broward County, Florida served by the 954 prefix](/images/blog/what-is-954-area-code.webp)

The **954 area code** is a phone code under the North American Numbering Plan. It covers only Broward County, Florida. Broward has two area codes in total. The other is 754, an overlay added in 2002. Regulators added it to relieve a shortage of numbers.

The **954 area code** launched on September 11, 1995. It split off from the original 305 area code. That code had covered all of Florida since 1947. South Florida grew fast in the late 1980s and early 1990s. New residents and new phones, pagers, and fax machines drove the demand.

Every call in **area code 954** runs on Eastern Time. That means UTC−5 in standard time and UTC−4 in Daylight Saving. Because 954 and 754 overlap, every call in Broward County needs 10-digit dialing.

## A Brief History of the 954 Area Code

![Timeline illustration of the 1995 split from 305 and the 2002 addition of the 754 overlay](/images/blog/history-954-area-code.webp)

The **954 area code** was born out of need. Florida's 305 code had served the whole state since 1947. Decades of growth used up its numbers. Splits in the 1980s and early 1990s shrank 305 to the Miami-Fort Lauderdale area. Even that smaller area ran low by the mid-1990s.

Regulators ordered another split. On September 11, 1995, the **954 area code** went live. It took over Broward County. Miami-Dade kept the 305 code. The plan was meant to last for decades. But South Florida kept growing.

Within five years, 954 ran low again. Mobile phones and other devices drove the surge. The Florida Public Service Commission approved an overlay plan in 2000. Area code 754 launched on April 1, 2002. Both codes have run side by side since then.

Today, the **954 area code** is still the older and better-known of the two. It is part of the region's identity.
