## What Is the 303 Area Code?

Area code 303 is one of the original area codes from 1947, assigned to the entire state of Colorado when the North American Numbering Plan launched. As Colorado's population grew — and particularly as the Denver metro exploded in population through the 1990s and 2000s — the demand for new numbers outpaced what 303 alone could supply.

Area code 720 was added as an overlay for the same geographic territory in 1998. Today, both 303 and 720 serve the Denver metro area, and ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls. The 303 code also serves Boulder and the surrounding region.
