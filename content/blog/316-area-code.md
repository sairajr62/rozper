## What Is the 316 Area Code?

Area code 316 is one of the original area codes from 1947, assigned to Kansas when the North American Numbering Plan first launched. For decades, 316 covered all of Kansas. As Kansas City grew and the eastern half of the state required more numbers, area code 913 was created in 1957 for northeastern Kansas (including the Kansas City suburbs). Area code 620 was then split off in 2001 to serve the southern and western portions of the state.

Today, 316 serves the Wichita metropolitan area and south-central Kansas — the region's commercial and industrial heart.
