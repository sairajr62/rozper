## What Is the 916 Area Code?

Area code 916 was one of the original North American Numbering Plan codes assigned in 1947, initially serving a large portion of Northern California. As the state's population and commercial activity grew through the latter half of the 20th century, the original 916 territory was gradually subdivided. In 1997, area code 530 was split off to serve the northeastern and northwestern rural portions of Northern California, leaving 916 concentrated on Sacramento and its immediate metro.

Today, 916 is one of two codes serving the Sacramento metro, alongside area code 279 (introduced in 2018 as an overlay to provide additional number capacity as the region continues to grow). Both 916 and 279 serve the same geographic territory, with 10-digit dialing required for all local calls within the overlay.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** Sacramento County and portions of adjacent counties, including Sacramento, Elk Grove, Folsom, Roseville, Rancho Cordova, Citrus Heights, and surrounding communities.
