## What Is the 937 Area Code?

![Southwest Ohio coverage map](/images/blog/937-image-1.png)

The 937 area code is the phone prefix for Southwest Ohio. Dayton sits at the center of it. The code covers a wide part of the state. It reaches everything outside the Cincinnati area. This corridor has used the code since 1996. It split off from Ohio's older 513 prefix that year. The state's people and phone demand had grown too big for one code.

In 2020, officials added the 326 area code as an overlay. Both codes now serve the exact same area. There is no service difference between the two. There is no map difference either. They are simply two number pools over one place.

Montgomery County anchors the region's economy. Wright-Patterson Air Force Base sits right in the county. It hosts a large share of the area's aerospace work. A strong healthcare sector operates alongside it, plus a growing logistics sector. Together they keep steady demand for local business lines across area code 937.

## How This Ohio Prefix Came to Be

![Timeline of the Ohio numbering split and overlay](/images/blog/937-image-3.png)

The 937 area code went into service on September 28, 1996. It split away from Ohio's older 513 prefix that day. This split gave Dayton its own number pool. Nearby counties got their own pool too. Southwest Ohio was now separate from Cincinnati.

More people kept moving to the area over the years, and phone number demand rose with them. Soon the region ran low on numbers again. A second geographic split would have forced people to change their numbers. So the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio chose a different path. In March 2020, it turned on the 326 area code as an overlay. Nobody had to change their existing number.

That overlay choice explains today's map. Area code 937 shares its exact area with 326. Both codes serve the exact same cities, with no difference in service or reach.

## Time Zone and the 326 Overlay for This Prefix

![Eastern Time zone coverage for Southwest Ohio](/images/blog/937-image-4.png)

Every number with the 937 area code runs on Eastern Time. So does every number on the newer 326 overlay. That means EST, or UTC-5, in winter. It means EDT, or UTC-4, from spring through fall.

The 326 overlay turned on back in 2020. Since then, every local call needs ten digits. Seven-digit dialing no longer connects, no matter which prefix the number carries.

Businesses often set up calls across time zones. A 9:00 a.m. meeting in Dayton starts differently elsewhere. It is 6:00 a.m. on the Pacific coast. It is 2:00 p.m. in London. Knowing the local time zone matters for planning. Knowing the dialing rule matters just as much. Together they help you avoid scheduling errors. They also help you avoid missed calls with Southwest Ohio partners.

**What is the 326 area code, and how does it relate to 937?**
