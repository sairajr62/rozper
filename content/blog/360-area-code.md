## What Is the 360 Area Code?

Area code 360 was created on January 15, 1995, when it split from the 206 area code. Before the split, 206 served all of Washington State west of the Cascades. As the Seattle metro area grew rapidly in the 1990s — fueled by Boeing, Microsoft, and the emerging technology sector — the demand for new numbers in the Seattle market made a split necessary. The Seattle metro retained 206, and the rest of western Washington was assigned the new 360 area code.

Since then, 360 has served western Washington outside of the Seattle metro consistently. Area code 564 was added as an overlay for the same territory in 2017.
