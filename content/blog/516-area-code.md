## Where Is the 516?

The **516 area code** covers Nassau County, the westernmost county of Long Island, directly east of Queens. It was established in 1951 when the original 212 area code was split to handle demand. In 1999, a second split created area code 631 for Suffolk County, leaving 516 exclusively for Nassau County.

![Where Is the 516?](/images/blog/516-image-1.webp)

In 2017, continued growth led to the 363 overlay. New lines in the same area may now receive 363 numbers, while existing **516 area code** numbers keep their prefix. Ten-digit dialing is required throughout the region.

For businesses, a number with area code 516 signals a Nassau County identity — distinct from the broader Long Island 631 market and clearly separate from the [917 area code](/blog/917-area-code) landscape.

## Protect Your 516 Number: Easy Ways to Avoid Scam Calls

Nassau County's high-income profile makes **516 area code** numbers attractive to scammers. Fraudsters target affluent suburban codes because residents are more engaged in financial transactions, professional services, and real estate — all high-value targets.

### Common Phone Scams in the 516 Area Code Region

**Neighbor spoofing:** Scammers use 516 prefixes to mimic local numbers, betting residents will answer a familiar-looking call. The script then begins — fake bank alerts, IRS demands, or utility shutoff threats.

**Real estate and mortgage scams:** In Nassau County's active market, scammers pose as mortgage lenders, title companies, or attorneys requesting wire transfers or financial data — often from spoofed 516 numbers.

**Healthcare and Medicare fraud:** With a significant senior population, scammers pose as Medicare representatives or providers requesting personal insurance and billing details.

**Business impostor scams:** Callers claim to represent county offices, chambers, or registries — then request fees for listings, permits, or filings that don't exist.

### How to Protect Yourself

- **Verify before acting:** End the call and dial the organization directly using a number from their official website — never the one that called you.
- **Register on the Do Not Call Registry:** Free registration at the [national Do Not Call registry](https://www.donotcall.gov/) cuts unsolicited commercial calls significantly.
- **Enable carrier-level call screening:** Most major carriers offer spam filtering — activate it in your account settings.
- **Never wire money by phone:** Legitimate banks, agencies, and title companies don't request wire transfers over unsolicited calls.
- **Report suspicious numbers:** File complaints with the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov and the FCC at consumercomplaints.fcc.gov.

Legitimate businesses share a responsibility to maintain caller trust: a verified, professional presence — consistent name, listed number, predictable call patterns — signals that your 516 area code number is genuine.
