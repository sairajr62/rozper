## What Is the 516 Area Code?

Area code 516 is one of the original area codes assigned when the North American Numbering Plan launched in 1947. It has served Nassau County, Long Island, since the beginning — making it one of the most established local identifiers in the entire region.

For decades, 516 also covered Suffolk County (eastern Long Island), but rapid population growth led to the creation of area code 631 for Suffolk County in 1999. Today, 516 serves Nassau County exclusively, with area code 934 added as an overlay in 2017 to handle continued demand for new numbers.
