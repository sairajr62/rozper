## History of the 312 Area Code

The **312 area code** was established in **1947** as one of the original 86 area codes under the North American Numbering Plan (NANP), covering the entire Chicago metro area.

**1996 — The 773 Split:** On October 11, 1996, Chicago neighborhoods outside downtown were reassigned to the new **773 area code**, leaving area code 312 to cover only the city's commercial heart.

**2009 — The 872 Overlay:** The **872 area code** was introduced as a full-city overlay, providing new numbers without reassigning existing ones. The [City of Chicago](https://www.chicago.gov/) remains one of the country's premier business centers, ensuring continued demand for 312 numbers across financial, legal, and technology sectors. Despite these additions, area code 312 has never lost its prestige, and March 12th is celebrated as **"312 Day"** by locals and businesses.

## Is a 312 Area Code Call a Scam?

The 312 area code is completely legitimate, but its high name recognition makes it a prime target for scammers using **caller ID spoofing** to make fraudulent calls appear local. **312 ranks among the top 10 most-spoofed area codes in the United States.** Businesses in high-recognition markets face the same challenge — those using [the 470 area code](/blog/470-area-code) in the Atlanta metro encounter comparable spoofing risks.

### Chicago-Specific Scams to Know

**IRS arrest warrant threats:** Callers posing as IRS agents claim an outstanding warrant and demand immediate payment — no legitimate IRS contact begins with an unsolicited call. **Law enforcement impersonation** and **fake debt collection** are also widespread, using urgency to pressure victims.

### How to Protect Yourself

Legitimate organizations never demand payment via gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency. Choose a VoIP provider that supports **STIR/SHAKEN** caller authentication — this verifies caller ID and significantly reduces spoofing effectiveness.
