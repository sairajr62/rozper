## History of the 312 Area Code

The **312 area code** began in **1947**. It was one of the original 86 area codes under the North American Numbering Plan (NANP). Back then, it covered the whole Chicago metro area.

**1996 — The 773 Split:** On October 11, 1996, Chicago neighborhoods outside downtown got a new code. They moved to the new **773 area code**. This left area code 312 to cover only the city's commercial heart.

**2009 — The 872 Overlay:** The **872 area code** arrived as a full-city overlay. It added new numbers without changing existing ones. The [City of Chicago](https://www.chicago.gov/) is still one of the country's top business centers. That keeps demand strong for 312 numbers in finance, law, and tech. Despite these changes, area code 312 has never lost its prestige. Locals and businesses even celebrate March 12th as **"312 Day."**

## Is a 312 Area Code Call a Scam?

The 312 area code is completely legitimate. But its fame makes it a prime target for scammers. They use **caller ID spoofing** to make fraudulent calls look local. **312 ranks among the top 10 most-spoofed area codes in the United States.** Other high-recognition markets face the same problem. Businesses using [the 470 area code](/blog/470-area-code) in the Atlanta metro see similar spoofing risks.

### Chicago-Specific Scams to Know

**IRS arrest warrant threats:** Callers pose as IRS agents. They claim you have an outstanding warrant and demand payment right away. No real IRS contact ever starts with an unsolicited call. **Fake police calls** and **fake debt collection** are also common. Both use fear to pressure victims.

### How to Protect Yourself

Legitimate organizations never demand payment by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency. Choose a VoIP provider that supports **STIR/SHAKEN** caller checks. This confirms caller ID and cuts down spoofing.
