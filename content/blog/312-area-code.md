## What Is the 312 Area Code?

Area code 312 is one of the original area codes from 1947, serving all of Illinois when the North American Numbering Plan first launched. As Chicago and Illinois grew, the code was progressively split and overlaid. Area code 708 was created in 1989 for the Chicago suburbs. Then in 1996, 773 was split off to serve Chicago's outer neighborhoods, leaving 312 to cover Chicago's central business district — the Loop, River North, the Magnificent Mile, and adjacent neighborhoods.

Today, 312 is one of the most geographically concentrated area codes in the country: it covers a relatively small downtown footprint, but that footprint is home to some of the highest business density in North America. Area code 872 was added as an overlay for Chicago in 2011.

**Areas and neighborhoods covered by 312:**

- The Loop
- River North
- Streeterville
- Gold Coast
- South Loop
- River West
- Greektown
- West Loop / Fulton Market
- Near North Side
- Near South Side
- Lincoln Park (portions)

**County covered:**

- Cook County (Chicago's central downtown core)

A 312 number is uniquely associated with Chicago's downtown business identity — recognized across the country as a marker of the city's commercial center.
