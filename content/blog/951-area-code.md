# 951 Area Code: A Complete Guide to California's Inland Empire

The Inland Empire is one of the fastest-growing parts of Southern California. It sits just east of Los Angeles. It drives growth in shipping, factories, and small business. Across most of its west side, one set of digits ties business together: the **951 area code**. This guide shows where the code sits on the map. It covers the cities it serves, its history, and how to get a number.

## Where Is the 951?

The **951 area code** serves western Riverside County in Southern California. It also covers a small slice of San Bernardino County. It sits in the heart of the Inland Empire. This is the inland area east of Los Angeles and north of San Diego. The area has grown fast. People and firms keep moving here from the coast, where costs run high. Cheap land helps drive this growth. So does easy freeway access. So does being close to two big cities. Together, these things have fueled steady growth over the past two decades.

Unlike many California area codes, the **951 area code** has no overlay. This means **seven-digit local dialing still works** here. Places like Los Angeles lost that perk long ago. For firms already serving Central Los Angeles, the [323 area code](/blog/323-area-code) covers Hollywood, East LA, and the South Gate area. ## From 909 to 951: A Short History

The **951 area code** is fairly young. It went into service on July 17, 2004. It was made by splitting off from the older 909 code. By the early 2000s, growth in the Inland Empire had used up the supply of 909 numbers. So officials carved western Riverside County into its own number plan. The split served the growing number of people in the area. The [Riverside County official site](https://www.riversideca.gov/) shows how this region became a key business hub of the Inland Empire.

Since then, **area code 951** has held its own. It has not needed an overlay or a further split. This shows the first plan gave the region enough room to grow.

## What Time Zone Is the 951 Area Code In?

The whole **951 area code** region runs on Pacific Time. That's Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8) in winter and Pacific Daylight Time (UTC−7) in summer. When you plan calls or support hours for a 951 number, use Pacific Time. It runs three hours behind the East Coast. It also matches the clock in Los Angeles and San Diego.
