# 951 Area Code: A Complete Guide to California's Inland Empire

The Inland Empire is one of Southern California's fastest-growing economic regions — a logistics, manufacturing, and small-business engine sitting just east of Los Angeles. Across most of its western half, business runs on one set of digits: the **951 area code**. This guide covers where the code sits on the map, the cities it serves, its history, and how to claim a number.

## Where Is the 951?

The **951 area code** serves western Riverside County in Southern California, along with a small slice of San Bernardino County. It sits in the heart of the Inland Empire — the inland region east of Los Angeles and north of San Diego that has grown rapidly as residents and businesses move away from the coast's high cost of living. Affordable land, strong freeway access, and proximity to two major metros have fueled relentless commercial expansion over the past two decades.

Unlike many California area codes, the **951 area code** has no overlay, meaning **seven-digit local dialing still works** within the region — a convenience that overlay markets like Los Angeles lost long ago. For businesses already serving Central Los Angeles, the [323 area code](/blog/323-area-code) covers Hollywood, East LA, and the South Gate corridor, while 951 extends that reach eastward into the Inland Empire.

## From 909 to 951: A Short History

The **951 area code** is relatively young. It went into service on July 17, 2004, created by splitting off from the older 909 code. By the early 2000s, the Inland Empire's rapid population and business growth had drained the available supply of 909 numbers, so regulators carved western Riverside County into its own numbering plan. The split served the growing population of western Riverside County, a region the [Riverside County official site](https://www.riversideca.gov/) has developed into a key commercial hub of the Inland Empire.

Since then, **area code 951** has held its own without requiring an overlay or a further split — a sign that the original plan gave the region sufficient room to grow.

## What Time Zone Is the 951 Area Code In?

The entire **951 area code** region runs on Pacific Time — Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8) in winter and Pacific Daylight Time (UTC−7) during daylight saving. When scheduling calls or support hours for a 951 number, plan around Pacific Time, which runs three hours behind the East Coast and on the same clock as Los Angeles and San Diego.

## Is the 951 Area Code a Scam?

A 951 number on your caller ID is not a red flag on its own. The **951 area code** is a legitimate, geographically assigned Southern California code tied to a real and populous region, used daily by thousands of residents, businesses, and service providers.

The concern common to every area code is spoofing. Scammers can fake a local-looking 951 number to increase the odds you answer, but the prefix only confirms that a call claims to originate from western Riverside County — it says nothing about the caller's intent. The safest habit is to evaluate the specific number, let unfamiliar calls go to voicemail, and return only those you can verify. The 949 area code covers neighboring Orange County just west of Corona, and knowing these distinctions helps businesses and consumers identify where incoming calls originate. As with any Southern California prefix, residents should be especially cautious of unsolicited calls demanding immediate payment, since legitimate agencies and utilities rarely, if ever, ask for payment during a first phone contact.
