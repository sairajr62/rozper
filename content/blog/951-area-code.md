# 951 Area Code: A Complete Guide to California's Inland Empire

The Inland Empire is one of Southern California's fastest-growing economic regions — a logistics, manufacturing, and small-business engine sitting just east of Los Angeles. Across most of its western half, business runs on one set of digits: the **951 area code**. For any company trying to reach customers in Riverside, Corona, or Temecula, a 951 number signals "we're local here." This guide covers where the code sits on the map, the cities it serves, its history, and how to claim a number.

## Where Is the 951 Area Code?

The **951 area code** serves western Riverside County in Southern California, along with a small slice of San Bernardino County. It sits in the heart of the Inland Empire — the inland region east of Los Angeles and north of San Diego that has grown rapidly as residents and businesses move away from the coast's high cost of living. Affordable land, strong freeway access, and proximity to two major metros have fueled relentless commercial expansion over the past two decades.

Unlike many California area codes, the **951 area code** has no overlay. It is the single code across its western Riverside County footprint, which means **seven-digit local dialing still works** within the region — a convenience that overlay markets like Los Angeles lost long ago.

## Key Cities in the 951 Area Code

The **951 area code** covers a string of cities that together form a major commercial corridor. A few stand out as anchors of the local economy:

- **Riverside** — the county seat and commercial core, home to government offices, healthcare systems, and the University of California, Riverside.
- **Moreno Valley** — one of the region's fastest-growing cities and a critical logistics and distribution hub.
- **Corona** — a business gateway on the Orange County border with a growing base of tech and manufacturing companies.
- **Temecula** — a tourism-driven wine country destination drawing visitors from across the Southwest.

Beyond those four, the **951 area code** also reaches Murrieta, Hemet, Menifee, Perris, Lake Elsinore, Eastvale, San Jacinto, Beaumont, Wildomar, and Banning, among others. Together these communities form one of California's most commercially active inland corridors, with strengths in warehousing, healthcare, retail, and professional services.

## From 909 to 951: A Short History

The **951 area code** is relatively young. It went into service on July 17, 2004, created by splitting off from the older 909 code. By the early 2000s, the Inland Empire's rapid population and business growth had drained the available supply of 909 numbers, so regulators carved western Riverside County into its own numbering plan under the new **area code 951** prefix. The split was administered to serve the growing population of western Riverside County, a region the [City of Riverside](https://www.riversideca.gov/) has developed into a key commercial hub of the Inland Empire.

Since then, **area code 951** has held its own without requiring an overlay or a further split — a sign that the original plan gave the region sufficient room to grow.

## What Time Zone Is the 951 Area Code In?

The entire **951 area code** region runs on Pacific Time. That means Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8) in the winter months and Pacific Daylight Time (UTC−7) during daylight saving time. When scheduling calls or support hours for a 951 number, plan around Pacific Time — three hours behind the East Coast, and the same clock as Los Angeles and San Diego.

## Is the 951 Area Code a Scam?

A 951 number appearing on your caller ID is not a red flag on its own. The **951 area code** is a legitimate, geographically assigned Southern California code tied to a real and populous region. Thousands of residents, businesses, and service providers use it every day for routine calls.

The concern — common to every area code — is spoofing. Scammers can fake a local-looking 951 number to increase the odds you answer. The prefix only confirms that a call claims to originate from western Riverside County; it says nothing about the caller's intent. The safest habit is to evaluate the specific number, let unfamiliar calls go to voicemail, and return only those you can verify — rather than trusting or dismissing any area code wholesale.

The 949 area code covers neighboring Orange County — the communities just west of Corona — making it another prefix worth recognizing. Similarly, [the 424 area code](/blog/424-area-code) serves Los Angeles coastal communities on the other side of the metro, and knowing these distinctions helps businesses and consumers identify where incoming calls originate.
