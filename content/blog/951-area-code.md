### Common Phone Scams to Watch For

Scammers constantly target businesses and individuals. Key tactics include:

- **Caller ID Spoofing:** Calls falsely appear from local 951 area code numbers or trusted sources to deceive you.
- **Vishing (Voice Phishing):** Deceptive calls aim to steal sensitive business or personal data through manipulation.
- **Tech Support Scams:** Fraudsters offer fake IT help, often trying to gain system access or illicit payments.
- **'One-Ring' Scams:** Brief calls designed to bait you into calling back costly premium-rate international numbers.
- **Government Impersonation:** Callers falsely pose as officials (e.g., IRS) demanding urgent payments or information.
