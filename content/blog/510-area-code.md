## What Is the 510 Area Code?

Area code 510 was established on September 2, 1991, as a split from area code 415. Prior to the split, 415 had served the entire San Francisco Bay Area — San Francisco, the East Bay, and the Peninsula. As the Bay Area's population and commercial density grew through the 1980s, the 415 number pool became insufficient, and 510 was carved out to serve the East Bay while 415 was retained for San Francisco and Marin County.

In 2002, the southern portion of 510's original territory — Fremont, Hayward, and adjacent communities in the southern Alameda County — was further separated into area code 925/additional codes. Today, 510 covers Alameda and Contra Costa counties, including Oakland and Berkeley.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** Alameda County (Oakland, Berkeley, Fremont, Hayward, San Leandro, Alameda, Emeryville, Piedmont) and portions of Contra Costa County.
