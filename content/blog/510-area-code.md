## What Is the 510 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 510 area code was created in 1991 when the original 415 area code was split. In 1998, the 925 area code was carved out for the inland eastern East Bay. In 2019, the 341 area code was introduced as an overlay — making ten-digit dialing standard for all local calls.

### Geographic Coverage

The 510 area code primarily serves Alameda County and western portions of Contra Costa County.

**Major Cities and Communities in the 510 Area Code:**

- **Oakland** — The largest city in Alameda County; a major port, cultural hub, and growing tech corridor
- **Berkeley** — Home to UC Berkeley and a globally recognized innovation ecosystem
- **Fremont** — A manufacturing and technology center in southern Alameda County
- **Hayward** — A significant suburban and commercial hub
- **Richmond** — A key industrial city in western Contra Costa County
- **Alameda, San Leandro, Union City, Newark** — Key commercial and residential communities
- **El Cerrito, San Pablo, Pinole** — Communities in Contra Costa County
- **Castro Valley, San Lorenzo** — Unincorporated areas in Alameda County
