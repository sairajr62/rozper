## What Is the 352 Area Code?

Area code 352 was created on April 2, 1995, when it split from area code 904 that had served northeastern and north-central Florida. As Florida's population grew — particularly in the Gainesville and Ocala areas — 904 became insufficient for the region's needs. The split assigned 352 to north-central Florida while 904 retained coverage of Jacksonville and the northeast corner of the state.

The 352 area code serves a geographically large territory covering the interior of north-central Florida, from the Georgia border south to The Villages and from the Gulf Coast inland.
