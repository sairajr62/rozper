## What Is the 501 Area Code?

The 501 area code is one of the original telephone area codes established under the North American Numbering Plan (NANP), introduced on January 1, 1947. At launch, it covered the entire state of Arkansas.

Population growth triggered two splits:

- **1997:** Area code 870 was created to serve eastern and southern Arkansas
- **2002:** Area code 479 was carved out for northwestern Arkansas, covering Fayetteville and Fort Smith

Today, 501 covers **central Arkansas** — the political and economic core of the state. All calls within the 501 region require ten-digit dialing, and the code operates in the **Central Time Zone (CT)** — UTC-6 standard, UTC-5 during daylight saving.

## Where Does the 501 Area Code Cover?

The 501 area code spans central Arkansas, from the state capital along the Arkansas River Valley north and west through the Ouachita Mountain foothills.

**Major cities served:** Little Rock (state capital), North Little Rock, Conway, Hot Springs, Benton, Cabot, Searcy, Sheridan, and Morrilton.

**Counties served:** Pulaski, Faulkner, Garland, Saline, Lonoke, White, Cleburne, Conway, Hot Spring, Perry, and Van Buren counties.

**Population covered:** Approximately 1.2 million residents — around 40% of Arkansas's total population. For any business targeting Arkansas consumers, the 501 region is the state's highest-density market.
