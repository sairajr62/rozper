## What Is the 501 Area Code?

The 501 area code is one of the original telephone area codes established under the North American Numbering Plan (NANP), introduced on January 1, 1947. At launch, it covered the entire state of Arkansas — making it the oldest and most recognized phone code in the state.

Over the decades, population growth triggered two splits:

- **1997:** Area code 870 was created to serve eastern and southern Arkansas
- **2002:** Area code 479 was carved out for northwestern Arkansas, covering Fayetteville, Fort Smith, and the I-49 corridor

Today, 501 covers **central Arkansas**, the political and economic core of the state. All calls within the 501 region require ten-digit dialing, and the code operates in the **Central Time Zone (CT)** — UTC-6 standard, UTC-5 during daylight saving time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What cities are covered by the 501 area code?**
The 501 area code covers central Arkansas, including Little Rock, North Little Rock, Conway, Hot Springs, Benton, Cabot, Searcy, and communities across Pulaski, Faulkner, Garland, Saline, and surrounding counties.

**Is 501 the only area code in Arkansas?**
No. Arkansas currently has three area codes: 501 (central), 870 (eastern and southern), and 479 (northwestern, including Fayetteville and Fort Smith). The 501 code is the original, established in 1947 when a single code served the entire state.

**Do I need an Arkansas address to get a 501 number?**
No. Through a VoIP provider, any business can obtain a 501 number and route calls to any device or location worldwide. There is no requirement to maintain a physical presence in Arkansas.

**Will a 501 number help with local SEO?**
Yes. Listing a 501 area code number in your Google Business Profile and local directories signals that your business serves the central Arkansas market, which can improve visibility in local searches for Little Rock, Conway, and Hot Springs.

**Are 501 calls associated with spam?**
Like any widely-used area code, the 501 prefix has appeared in robocall campaigns, making some residents cautious about unknown 501 numbers. The most effective counter is consistent caller ID with a recognizable business name, paired with calling during reasonable hours and following up with a text if calls go unanswered.

**Can I use a 501 number for texting as well as calls?**
Yes. VoIP providers that support SMS and MMS allow your 501 number to send and receive text messages — increasingly important for customer engagement in retail, healthcare, hospitality, and service businesses throughout central Arkansas.

**How quickly can I activate a 501 number?**
With a modern VoIP platform, activation typically takes minutes. You choose your number, configure basic routing, and the line is live — no waiting for hardware installation or technician appointments.
