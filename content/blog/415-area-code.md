## What Is the 415 Area Code?

Area code 415 is one of the original area codes established in 1947, when the North American Numbering Plan first organized telephone numbering across the continent. At that time, 415 covered all of Northern California west of the Sierra Nevada — an enormous territory that included San Francisco, the Bay Area, and much of the surrounding region.

As Northern California's population grew, the code was progressively split: 510 was split off in 1991 to serve the East Bay, 408 was split off earlier in 1984 to serve the South Bay, and 650 was split off in 1997 to serve the Peninsula south of San Francisco. Today, 415 serves San Francisco proper and Marin County directly north of the Golden Gate Bridge. Area code 628 was added as an overlay in 2015.

**Cities and areas covered by 415:**

- San Francisco (all neighborhoods and districts)
- Mill Valley
- San Rafael
- Novato
- Sausalito
- Corte Madera
- Tiburon
- Belvedere
- Fairfax
- San Anselmo

**Counties covered:**

- San Francisco County
- Marin County

The 415 area code serves the city of San Francisco and Marin County — one of the most commercially and culturally significant geographic footprints in the country.
