## What Is the 347 Area Code?

Area code 347 was introduced on January 1, 1999, as an overlay to the existing 718 area code that had served New York City's outer boroughs since 1984. As the demand for new numbers in Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island outpaced what 718 alone could supply, 347 was added as an overlay — meaning it covers exactly the same geographic territory as 718, and ten-digit dialing is required for all local calls.

More recently, area code 929 was added as a second overlay for the same territory in 2011, reflecting the continued explosive growth in demand for New York City phone numbers.

**Boroughs and areas covered by 347:**

- Brooklyn (Kings County)
- Queens (Queens County)
- The Bronx (Bronx County)
- Staten Island (Richmond County)

**Notable neighborhoods and areas:**

- Williamsburg and Bushwick (Brooklyn)
- Park Slope and Crown Heights (Brooklyn)
- Astoria and Long Island City (Queens)
- Flushing and Jamaica (Queens)
- Fordham and Riverdale (Bronx)
- St. George and Stapleton (Staten Island)

A 347 number is recognized everywhere as a New York City number — specifically associated with the outer boroughs that make up the majority of the city's population and commercial activity.
