## What Is the 949 Area Code?

Area code 949 was activated on April 18, 1998, when the southern Orange County cities were carved out of area code 714. The split gave the region a distinct phone identity and created a fresh pool of numbers for one of California's fastest-growing markets at the time — southern OC was undergoing rapid residential and commercial development driven by master-planned communities like Irvine and new coastal amenity markets in Newport Beach and Laguna Beach.

Unlike many California regions that have since absorbed overlay codes, southern Orange County still runs on 949 alone — making 949 one of the more stable and distinctive single codes in the state, and a 949 number a clearly recognizable regional asset.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** Southern Orange County — a coastal and inland corridor south of the older 714 territory, spanning from beach communities along the Pacific to master-planned communities in the foothills and canyons.
