## What Is the 949 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 949 area code is a product of Orange County's explosive growth. For decades, area code 714 served the entire county. As Orange County's population boomed and its corporate base expanded — drawing technology firms, financial services companies, and a thriving professional services sector — available 714 numbers ran critically low. On April 18, 1998, southern Orange County was carved out and given its own code: 949.

Unlike many California regions that have since absorbed an overlay, southern Orange County still runs on 949 alone — there is no second code sharing its territory. That makes 949 one of the more distinctive standalone codes in the state, and a 949 number a recognizably regional asset without the ambiguity of overlapping codes.

The 949 area code serves southern Orange County — a coastal and inland stretch spanning from beach communities along the Pacific to master-planned communities in the foothills.

**Major Cities and Communities in the 949 Area Code:**

- **Coastal cities:** Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, and San Clemente — blending luxury residential, hospitality, and high-end retail along one of California's most recognizable coastlines
- **Inland cities:** Irvine, Mission Viejo, Lake Forest, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, and San Juan Capistrano — home to major corporate campuses, the Irvine Company's business parks, and a dense base of professional services, healthcare, and technology firms

For a business, that diversity is the point: a single 949 number speaks credibly to a venture-backed startup in Irvine, a wealth management firm in Newport Beach, and a medical practice in Mission Viejo alike.
