## What Is the 949 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 949 area code is a product of Orange County's explosive growth. For decades, area code 714 served the entire county. As the region's corporate base expanded and available 714 numbers ran low, southern Orange County was carved out on April 18, 1998, and given its own code: 949.

Unlike many California regions, southern Orange County still runs on 949 alone — no second code shares its territory. That makes 949 one of the more distinctive standalone codes in the state.

The 949 area code operates in the **Pacific Time Zone** (UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time).

**Major Cities in the 949 Area Code:**

- **Coastal:** Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, and San Clemente — luxury residential, hospitality, and high-end retail along California's southern coastline
- **Inland:** Irvine, Mission Viejo, Lake Forest, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, and San Juan Capistrano — major corporate campuses and a dense base of professional services, healthcare, and tech firms

A single 949 number speaks credibly to a startup in Irvine, a wealth management firm in Newport Beach, and a medical practice in Mission Viejo alike.
