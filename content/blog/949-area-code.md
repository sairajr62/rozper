## What Is the 949 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

The 949 area code is a product of Orange County's explosive growth. For decades, area code 714 served the entire county. As Orange County's population boomed and its business base expanded — drawing corporate relocations, technology firms, and a thriving professional services sector — the available pool of 714 numbers ran critically low.

On April 18, 1998, the southern Orange County cities were carved out and given their own code: 949. The split gave the region a distinct identity on the phone and created a fresh pool of numbers for one of California's fastest-growing markets.

Unlike many California regions that have since absorbed an overlay, southern Orange County still runs on 949 alone — there is no second code sharing its territory. That makes 949 one of the more distinctive standalone codes in the state, and a 949 number a recognizably regional asset rather than one shared across two overlapping codes.

### Geographic Coverage

The 949 area code serves southern Orange County — a coastal and inland stretch south of the older 714 territory, spanning from beach communities along the Pacific to master-planned communities in the foothills.
