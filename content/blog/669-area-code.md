## What Is the 669 Area Code?

Area code 669 was introduced on September 4, 2012, as an overlay for area code 408, which had served San Jose and Silicon Valley since 1984. By the early 2010s, the original 408 number pool was approaching exhaustion — a direct consequence of the region's explosive technology-sector growth, which had driven massive demand for mobile lines, corporate phone systems, and business VoIP services. The 669 overlay was added to extend the available number supply for the same geographic territory.

The 408/669 overlay means both codes cover San Jose and Silicon Valley simultaneously. Businesses can operate 669 numbers with full local equivalency to 408 numbers — there is no geographic or status distinction between the two codes. All local calls within the overlay require 10-digit dialing.

**Time zone:** Pacific Time (PT) — UTC−8 standard, UTC−7 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** Santa Clara County — San Jose, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara, Milpitas, Cupertino, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Morgan Hill, and Gilroy.
