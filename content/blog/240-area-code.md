# 240 Area Code: Maryland's Location, Cities, and How to Get a Number

Picture a customer in Rockville. They look at their phone before they pick up. A number that starts with 240 feels like a neighbor. An unfamiliar one feels like a sales pitch. That small difference is why the **240 area code** still matters for anyone doing business in Maryland. This guide covers what you need to know. You'll learn where the code sits on the map, which cities it reaches, its time zone, why it overlaps with 301 and 227, and how to claim a number of your own.

## Where Is the 240 Area Code?

The **240 area code** serves western and central Maryland. It covers Maryland's slice of the Greater Washington, D.C. metro. It stretches west into Appalachian communities near the state's panhandle. It also dips south into the counties below the capital. If you know [the 302 area code](/blog/302-area-code), you'll notice a difference. That code covers neighboring Delaware as one statewide code. Maryland took a different path. It chose to layer several codes over the same map instead of splitting it into separate zones.

Its coverage includes all of Allegany, Charles, Garrett, Montgomery, Prince George's, St. Mary's, and Washington counties. It also covers most of Frederick County, western Howard County, and small slices of Carroll and Anne Arundel counties. One detail trips people up. Some online listings tag it as a "Baltimore" code, but **area code 240** does **not** cover the city of Baltimore. Baltimore proper uses 410, 443, and 667.

## Cities Covered by the 240 Area Code

The **240 area code** reaches a long list of well-known Maryland towns and cities. Among the biggest and best known are:

- **Montgomery County:** Rockville, Bethesda, Silver Spring, Gaithersburg, Germantown, Potomac, Montgomery Village
- **Frederick County:** Frederick, the county's main business hub
- **Prince George's County:** College Park, Greenbelt, Landover
- **Western Maryland:** Hagerstown, Cumberland
- **Southern Maryland:** Waldorf and surrounding St. Mary's and Charles county towns

That spread means a single 240 number can feel "local" to a tech worker in Bethesda, a student in College Park, and a small-business owner in Hagerstown alike.

## A Short History of the 240 Area Code

The **240 area code** went live on June 1, 1997. It was not carved out of new territory. Instead, it was layered directly on top of the existing 301 region as an overlay. Maryland's suburbs were running out of available 301 numbers. Rather than split the area in two and force half the population to change their numbers, regulators introduced 240 to cover the identical map.

That is why longtime residents often keep a 301 number while newer lines get 240, even on the same street. A third code, 227, was later added to the same overlay to keep the supply of numbers flowing. So today, 301, **area code 240**, and 227 all cover the exact same ground in western and central Maryland. Number assignments in this region are coordinated with the [State of Maryland](https://www.maryland.gov/). That agency offers business and rule-related help for the whole western and central Maryland corridor. This keeps new codes rolling out in a steady, fair way.

One practical result: **ten-digit dialing is mandatory**. Three codes share one footprint. So even a call across the street needs the full area code plus the seven-digit number.

## Is a 240 Call Safe? Scams and Cell Phone Myths

Two questions come up all the time, so let's clear them up.

**Is 240 a scam code?** No. This is a real Maryland code tied to a real place. Like any area code, bad actors can spoof it. But the prefix itself is real and tied to a true region. A 240 call is no more "automatically" suspicious than a call from any other U.S. area code. Judge the caller, not the prefix.

**Is 240 only for cell phones?** No. The 240 prefix is assigned across home landlines, mobile phones, businesses, universities, hospitals, and VoIP services. It is a general-purpose geographic code, not a wireless-only one. If you get an unexpected 240 call, the safest move is to let it go to voicemail and search the number online before you respond. If you also operate in areas served by [the 203 area code](/blog/203-area-code), those same tips for judging unfamiliar callers apply equally in that Connecticut region.

## What Time Zone Does 240 Use?

The entire 240 area code region sits in the Eastern Time Zone. During the winter months, that means Eastern Standard Time (UTC−5). From spring through fall, it shifts to Eastern Daylight Time (UTC−4). If you plan calls, support hours, or outbound sales runs, treat every 240 number as Eastern Time. That's the same clock as New York, Atlanta, and Washington, D.C.
