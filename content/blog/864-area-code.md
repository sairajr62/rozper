## 864 History, Coverage, and Cities

For 48 years, 803 was the only area code in South Carolina. Then the Upstate grew. Its population and business base got bigger, and numbers ran short. So the region split off into a new area code, 864, on December 3, 1995. By the 2020s, 864 numbers were running low again. On August 19, 2024, the state added the 821 overlay. It covers the same area. It also adds a fresh pool of numbers. It made ten-digit dialing mandatory across the region too.

An older 864 number stands out a bit more today, since new lines may now carry the 821 code instead. The main hubs in the 864 area are Greenville, Spartanburg, and Anderson. Nearby communities include Greer, Mauldin, Clemson, and Gaffney.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where is the 864 area code located?**
The 864 area code covers the Upstate region, the western third of South Carolina. It includes Greenville, Spartanburg, and Anderson. It also covers roughly a dozen nearby counties and cities, such as Greer, Mauldin, Clemson, and Gaffney. The area stretches from the Blue Ridge foothills toward the state's midlands.

**What is the 821 area code, and how is it related to 864?**
821 is an overlay code added on August 19, 2024. It covers the exact same area as 864. It was added to create more available numbers. It didn't change anyone's existing line or split the region. So new numbers in the Upstate may now carry either an 864 or 821 code. Both serve the same territory.

**Do I have to dial ten digits for 864 numbers?**
Yes. Because 864 and 821 cover the same territory, you must dial ten digits for every call, even local ones. Always dial the full 864 area code, then the seven-digit number. This applies even when calling a neighbor or a business down the street.

**What time zone is the 864 area code in?**
The 864 area code is in the Eastern Time Zone. That's EST (UTC-5) in standard time and EDT (UTC-4) during daylight saving time. When you schedule calls to the region, plan around standard Eastern business hours. That way you reach contacts during their working day.
