## What Is the 864 Area Code? History, Coverage, and Cities

For 48 years, 803 was the only area code in South Carolina. As numbers ran short, the Upstate was split off into the new 864 area code on December 3, 1995. The 864 pool ran low again by the 2020s, prompting an overlay: the 821 area code, effective August 19, 2024. Both codes share the same geography — brand-new lines may carry 821, making an established 864 a quiet signal of regional longevity.
