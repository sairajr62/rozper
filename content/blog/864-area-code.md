## What Is the 864 Area Code?

Area code 864 was established in 1995 as a split from area code 803, which had previously served all of South Carolina. As the state's population grew and commercial demand for phone numbers increased through the early 1990s, the single-state code could no longer supply enough numbers. The split assigned 864 to the Upstate (Greenville-Spartanburg corridor and surrounding counties) while 803 was retained for the Midlands and Lowcountry.

**Time zone:** Eastern Time (ET) — UTC−5 standard, UTC−4 during Daylight Saving Time.

**Geographic coverage:** The northwestern portion of South Carolina — Greenville County, Spartanburg County, Anderson County, Pickens County, Oconee County, Cherokee County, Union County, Laurens County, Abbeville County, McCormick County, and Edgefield County.
