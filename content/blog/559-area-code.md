## History of the 559 Area Code

![California Number Plan Pyramid Diagram Under the NANP](/images/blog/559-image-3.webp)

### California's 209: The Original Central Valley Code

Before area code 559 existed, Central California was covered by **area code 209**, which served the San Joaquin Valley since the North American Numbering Plan launched in 1947. The mobile phone boom and proliferation of pager and fax lines in the 1990s drove demand, pushing 209 toward exhaustion.

### November 14, 1998: 559 Created

On **November 14, 1998**, area code 559 was created to serve Fresno, Madera, Kings, and Tulare counties after the CNCA flagged 209 as approaching exhaustion. The northern portion — Stockton and Modesto — retained the 209 prefix.

### March 26, 2025: The 357 Overlay Launches

By the 2020s, growth in mobile devices, VoIP, and IoT pushed the 559 numbering supply toward exhaustion. The CPUC approved the **357 area code** as an overlay responding to the sustained growth the [City of Fresno](https://www.fresno.gov/) has driven as the commercial anchor of California's Central Valley. The new code went live on **March 26, 2025**, with **mandatory 10-digit dialing** effective in Q1 2025.

## 559 Area Code Time Zone

The **559 area code** is in the **Pacific Time Zone (PT)**: PST (UTC−8) from early November to mid-March, and PDT (UTC−7) from mid-March to early November.

### Cross-Timezone Calling Guide

**East Coast (ET):** 3 hours ahead — call by 9 AM PT. **Central Time (CT):** 2 hours ahead with solid overlap. **Mountain Time (MT):** 1 hour ahead, the easiest pair for coordination.

## Is a 559 Area Code Call a Scam?

The **559 area code** is legitimate, but like all US area codes it is subject to caller ID spoofing. Several scam types are documented in the region.

**Fake Amazon Purchase Alerts:** Robocalls claim an expensive purchase was placed on the recipient's account — Amazon does not initiate unsolicited robocalls about specific purchases.

**PG&E Utility Shutoff Threats:** Scammers claim to be PG&E employees threatening imminent shutoff unless immediate payment is made. PG&E uses written notices for legitimate disconnections.

**Medicare "Final Notice" Scams:** Callers claim Medicare eligibility is about to be suspended. Real Medicare representatives do not make unsolicited calls claiming imminent benefit suspension.

**Social Security Administration Impersonation:** Spoofed voicemails claim suspicious SSN activity with legal action pending — the SSA does not leave threatening voicemails.

**Law Enforcement Impersonation:** Scammers have impersonated the **Fresno County Sheriff's Office** claiming a warrant and demanding payment. The Sheriff's Office does not solicit money by phone.

Never pay fines or share account information in response to unsolicited calls.
