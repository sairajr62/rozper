## What Is the 561 Area Code?

The 561 area code was carved out of the old 407 region on **May 13, 1996**. South Florida's population was booming, and phone numbers were running short. For years, this prefix served Palm Beach County well on its own.

## Area Code 561 History and the 728 Overlay

![Timeline showing the 561 split from 407 and the 728 overlay](/images/blog/561-area-code-561-history-and-the-728-overlay.webp)

This prefix was carved out of the old 407 region on May 13, 1996. South Florida's population was booming. Phone numbers were running short. For years it served Palm Beach County on its own. Growth from mobile phones, fax lines, and other systems slowly used up the open number combos again.

Rather than force a hard geographic split, regulators approved 728 as an overlay. It sits on top of the same area. New lines in the region can now be issued under 728. Every existing local number keeps working just as it always has. Ten-digit dialing became a must as a result. No current customer had to change their contact card, though.

## 561 Area Code Time Zone

Palm Beach County sits in the **Eastern Time Zone**. The region runs on **EST** (UTC−5) from early November through mid-March. It runs on **EDT** (UTC−4) the rest of the year. Tourism, real estate, and finance firms in the county tend to follow ET business hours closely. Because of this, teams calling from the West Coast should plan outreach before 2 PM PT. That gives them the best shot at reaching South Florida offices before the day ends.

## Is a 561 Area Code Call a Scam?

Because the prefix is so well known, scammers use that trust the same way real firms do. Robocalls spoofing a 561 number often act like Social Security agents. Some act like law offices with fake lawsuits. Others act like "Google listing" collectors demanding fast payment. This trick is called neighbor spoofing. It copies a local number so residents feel more likely to answer. The [Federal Communications Commission](https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/spoofing) explains how this spoofing works. Caller ID alone cannot confirm who is really calling.

You may get a surprise call from this prefix about urgent legal or money trouble. If you do, do not call the number back. Look up the group's real contact info on your own. Confirm the story directly with them. Real agencies rarely demand instant payment. They rarely threaten arrest over the phone either.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What state and cities does the 561 area code cover?**
This prefix covers Palm Beach County, Florida, including West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Jupiter, Wellington, and Belle Glade. It runs from the Atlantic coast inland toward Lake Okeechobee and operates entirely on Eastern Time.

**When was this prefix created?**
It was created on May 13, 1996, when regulators split it away from the larger 407 area code to keep up with South Florida's rapid population growth during the 1990s.

**Is area code 561 an overlay area code?**
No, it is the original code for the region. Instead, 728 was added as an overlay on top of the same Palm Beach County territory once open combos ran low, so both prefixes now coexist there.

**Can I get a 561 area code number without living in Florida?**
Yes. Virtual number providers let businesses anywhere activate one and forward calls to any phone, VoIP system, or contact center, giving you a local Palm Beach County presence without a physical office.

**Are calls from this prefix always legitimate?**
Not necessarily. Scammers spoof it to appear local, often acting like government agencies or law firms. Always verify unexpected calls independently before sharing personal or payment information.
