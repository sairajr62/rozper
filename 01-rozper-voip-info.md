## What Rozper Does — Core Business

Rozper operates a unified communications and global voice platform built to support carriers, call centers, VoIP resellers, enterprises, and ISPs requiring reliable international voice connectivity and modern collaboration tools.

The platform delivers:
- UCaaS (Unified Communications as a Service)
- Carrier-grade wholesale voice termination (A-Z)
- SIP trunking
- Virtual phone numbers across 150+ countries
- Bulk SMS and multi-channel business messaging
- Inbound and outbound call center solutions

Through a globally distributed network engineered for 99.999% uptime, Rozper enables businesses to expand into new markets with local presence, unify hybrid teams in a single communications hub, and manage large-scale voice traffic with carrier-grade reliability — backed by 24/7 human expert support.
